# Calcium Fluoride, Claviceps Purpurea Sclerotium, Crocanthemum Canadense, Fraxinus Americana Bark, Fucus Vesiculosus and Thuja Occidentalis Leafy Twig

> **Note:** Official U.S. prescribing information, written for health professionals; very long sections are shortened. Take prescription medicines only as prescribed, and never start, stop or change a dose without asking your doctor or pharmacist. Not medical advice.

**Route:** oral  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Brand and product names:** Bm Bm36 Drops

## Uses

Uses Helps temporarily relieve: pelvic discomfort, abdominal bloating, and pressure; heavy discharge, spotting, and painful periods; discomfort during intercourse; and hormonal imbalance.

## Dosage

Directions Use as directed. Mix 10 drops with half a cup of water and drink immediately. Repeat this process 3–4 times daily. Ages 12 and older only.

## Warnings

Keep out of reach of children. If you have severe symptoms, consult a doctor before use.
