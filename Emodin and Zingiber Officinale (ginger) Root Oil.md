## Active ingredients

ZINGIBER OFFICINALE (GINGER) ROOT EXTRACT、POLYGONUM MULTIFLORUM EXTRACT

## Purpose

1、Rich in nutrients, it can effectively repair damaged hair, promote hair rejuvenation from the inside out, and rebuild a strong foundation for hair. 2、It can strengthen hair roots, regulate scalp oil balance, reduce hair loss, and protect scalp health. 3、It can directly act on hair follicles, deeply nourish them, stimulate the vitality of hair follicles, and accelerate hair growth.

## Uses

1、Clean and dry hair 2、Spray this product evenly on the scalp and hair 3、Massage your scalp and hair with your fingers for 2-3 minutes.
