## Active ingredients

MENTHA SUAVEOLENS LEAF、APIS MELLIFERA VENOM、ALOE VERA LEAF

## Purpose

Painlessly removes skin warts and moles; quickly removes skin tags and stimulates tissue regeneration; removes various types of warts; effectively dissolves warts and restores smooth skin.

## Uses

After cleansing and drying the skin, apply an appropriate amount of this product on warts or moles and massage gently to promote absorption.

## Warnings
