# Hypromelloses and Tetrahydrozoline Hydrochloride

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Dr. Shitong Photon Energy Eye Care Liquid

## Active ingredients

Tetrahydrozoline HCl 0.05% Hypromellose 0.3%

## Purpose

Redness reliever Ophthalmic Lubricant

## Uses

For daily eye care of people with eye discomfort caused by excessive eye use, eye fatigue,dryness, itching, soreness,distending pain, and mild eye irritation-induced redness.

## Warnings

For external use only.

### Do not use

if solution changes color or becomes cloudy

### Ask a doctor before use if

Ask Doctor Temporary mild stinging may appear after use and usually fades quickly on its own.If stinging lasts long, eye discharge increases abnormally or you have obvious discomfort, stop use right away and consult a healthcare provider promptly.

### When using this product

When Using If pregnant or breast-feeding,aska health professionl before use.

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

Stop Use Temporary mild stinging may appear after use and usually fades quickly on its own.If stinging lasts long, eye discharge increases abnormally or you have obvious discomfort, stop use right away and consult a healthcare provider promptly.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep Out Of Reach Of Children Keep this product out of reach of children and pets.

## Directions

Use 8 to 10 times per day. Made from pure plant extracts, suitable for long-term use. Finish within 10 days after opening. Not intended for inflammatory eye conditions.

## Other information

Do not freeze.Do not use if bottle is damaged.Store cool and dry, avoid direct sunlight.

## Inactive ingredients

Angelica Sinensis Root Extract,Cassia Obtusifolia Seed Extract,Clematis Chinensis Root Extract,Alpinia Officinarum Rhizome, Magnolia Liliflora Flower Extract.
