Magnesium Aluminosilicates 200 mg Magnesium Hydroxide 60 mg Simethicone 20 mg

## Purpose

Magnesium Aluminosilicates Antacid Magnesium Hydroxide Antacid Simethicone Antigas

## Uses

relieves these symptoms

- heartburn
- acid indigestion
- sour stomach
- upset stomach and gas associated with these symptoms
