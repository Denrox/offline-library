# Birth Weight

#### What is birth weight?

Birth weight is the first weight of your baby, taken just after being born. A low birth weight means that the baby is less than 5 pounds, 8 ounces. A high birth weight means that the baby is more than 8 pounds, 13 ounces.

#### What can cause low birth weight?

A baby with a low birth weight can be born too small, too early ([premature](https://medlineplus.gov/prematurebabies.html)), or both. This can happen for many different reasons. They include:

- [Health problems in the pregnant parent](https://medlineplus.gov/healthproblemsinpregnancy.html)
- [Taking certain medicines during pregnancy](https://medlineplus.gov/pregnancyandmedicines.html)
- [Substance use during pregnancy](https://medlineplus.gov/pregnancyandsubstanceuse.html)
- [Infections during pregnancy](https://medlineplus.gov/infectionsandpregnancy.html)
- Being pregnant with [multiple babies](https://medlineplus.gov/twinstripletsmultiplebirths.html)
- Problems with the placenta, the organ that brings oxygen and nutrients to the baby
- Having small parents
- [Genetic conditions](https://medlineplus.gov/geneticdisorders.html) in the baby
- [Birth defects](https://medlineplus.gov/birthdefects.html)

#### What problems can low birth weight cause?

Babies with low birth weight may be more at risk of certain health problems. They include immediate problems, such as:

- [Breathing problems](https://medlineplus.gov/breathingproblems.html)
- Infections
- [Hypoglycemia](https://medlineplus.gov/hypoglycemia.html) (low blood sugar)
- [Jaundice](https://medlineplus.gov/jaundice.html)
- Trouble keeping warm

There is also a higher risk of longer-term problems, including:

- Delayed motor and social development
- [Learning disabilities](https://medlineplus.gov/learningdisabilities.html)
- Certain health conditions as an adult, such as [heart disease](https://medlineplus.gov/heartdiseases.html), [high blood pressure](https://medlineplus.gov/highbloodpressure.html), [diabetes](https://medlineplus.gov/diabetes.html), and [obesity](https://medlineplus.gov/obesity.html)

#### What can cause high birth weight?

Causes of high birth weight can include:

- Having big parents
- [Diabetes in the pregnant parent](https://medlineplus.gov/diabetesandpregnancy.html)
- Certain genetic conditions
- Too much weight gain during pregnancy

#### What problems can high birth weight cause?

High birth weight can be a concern because it can make delivery of the baby difficult and raise the risk of birth injuries. The baby is at higher risk of problems with:

- [Blood sugar](https://medlineplus.gov/bloodglucose.html)
- Breathing problems
- Jaundice

#### Can birth weight problems be prevented?

You may be able to prevent some birth weight problems by:

- Getting regular [checkups during pregnancy](https://medlineplus.gov/prenatalcare.html)
- Getting the right amount of [nutrition](https://medlineplus.gov/pregnancyandnutrition.html) and calories during pregnancy
- Controlling your blood sugar if you have diabetes
- Avoiding substance use during pregnancy
