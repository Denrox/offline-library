# Birth Weight

#### What is birth weight?

Birth weight is the first weight of your baby, taken just after being born. A low birth weight means that the baby is less than 5 pounds, 8 ounces. A high birth weight means that the baby is more than 8 pounds, 13 ounces.

#### What can cause low birth weight?

A baby with a low birth weight can be born too small, too early ([premature](Premature%20Babies.md)), or both. This can happen for many different reasons. They include:

- [Health problems in the pregnant parent](Health%20Problems%20in%20Pregnancy.md)
- [Taking certain medicines during pregnancy](Pregnancy%20and%20Medicines.md)
- [Substance use during pregnancy](Pregnancy%20and%20Substance%20Use.md)
- [Infections during pregnancy](Infections%20and%20Pregnancy.md)
- Being pregnant with [multiple babies](Twins%2C%20Triplets%2C%20Multiple%20Births.md)
- Problems with the placenta, the organ that brings oxygen and nutrients to the baby
- Having small parents
- [Genetic conditions](Genetic%20Disorders.md) in the baby
- [Birth defects](Birth%20Defects.md)

#### What problems can low birth weight cause?

Babies with low birth weight may be more at risk of certain health problems. They include immediate problems, such as:

- [Breathing problems](Breathing%20Problems.md)
- Infections
- [Hypoglycemia](Hypoglycemia.md) (low blood sugar)
- [Jaundice](Jaundice.md)
- Trouble keeping warm

There is also a higher risk of longer-term problems, including:

- Delayed motor and social development
- [Learning disabilities](Learning%20Disabilities.md)
- Certain health conditions as an adult, such as [heart disease](Heart%20Diseases.md), [high blood pressure](High%20Blood%20Pressure.md), [diabetes](Diabetes.md), and [obesity](Obesity.md)

#### What can cause high birth weight?

Causes of high birth weight can include:

- Having big parents
- [Diabetes in the pregnant parent](Diabetes%20and%20Pregnancy.md)
- Certain genetic conditions
- Too much weight gain during pregnancy

#### What problems can high birth weight cause?

High birth weight can be a concern because it can make delivery of the baby difficult and raise the risk of birth injuries. The baby is at higher risk of problems with:

- [Blood sugar](Blood%20Glucose.md)
- Breathing problems
- Jaundice

#### Can birth weight problems be prevented?

You may be able to prevent some birth weight problems by:

- Getting regular [checkups during pregnancy](Prenatal%20Care.md)
- Getting the right amount of [nutrition](Pregnancy%20and%20Nutrition.md) and calories during pregnancy
- Controlling your blood sugar if you have diabetes
- Avoiding substance use during pregnancy
