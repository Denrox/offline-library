# Occupational Health

Occupational health problems occur at work or because of the kind of work you do. These problems can include:

- Cuts, [fractures](https://medlineplus.gov/fractures.html) (broken bones), and [sprains and strains](https://medlineplus.gov/sprainsandstrains.html)
- [Loss of limbs](https://medlineplus.gov/limbloss.html)
- Repetitive motion disorders
- [Hearing problems](https://medlineplus.gov/hearingdisordersanddeafness.html) caused by exposure to [noise](https://medlineplus.gov/noise.html)
- [Vision problems](https://medlineplus.gov/visionimpairmentandblindness.html)
- Illness caused by breathing, touching, or swallowing unsafe substances
- Illness caused by [exposure to radiation](https://medlineplus.gov/radiationexposure.html)
- Exposure to germs in [health care settings](https://medlineplus.gov/occupationalhealthforhealthcareproviders.html)

Good job safety and prevention practices can reduce your risk of these problems. Try to stay fit, reduce stress, set up your [work area](https://medlineplus.gov/ergonomics.html) properly, and use the right equipment and gear.
