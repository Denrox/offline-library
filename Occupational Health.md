# Occupational Health

Occupational health problems occur at work or because of the kind of work you do. These problems can include:

- Cuts, [fractures](Fractures.md) (broken bones), and [sprains and strains](Sprains%20and%20Strains.md)
- [Loss of limbs](Limb%20Loss.md)
- Repetitive motion disorders
- [Hearing problems](Hearing%20Disorders%20and%20Deafness.md) caused by exposure to [noise](Noise.md)
- [Vision problems](Vision%20Impairment%20and%20Blindness.md)
- Illness caused by breathing, touching, or swallowing unsafe substances
- Illness caused by [exposure to radiation](Radiation%20Exposure.md)
- Exposure to germs in [health care settings](Occupational%20Health%20for%20Health%20Care%20Providers.md)

Good job safety and prevention practices can reduce your risk of these problems. Try to stay fit, reduce stress, set up your [work area](Ergonomics.md) properly, and use the right equipment and gear.
