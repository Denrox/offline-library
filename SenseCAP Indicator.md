# SenseCAP Indicator

A 4-inch touchscreen device powered by dual MCUs—the ESP32 and RP2040—that supports Wi-Fi, BLE, and LoRa®.

### Specifications

- **MCU**
  - ESP32-S3 (Wi-Fi & Bluetooth) + RP2040
- **LoRa Transceiver**
  - Semtech SX1262
- **Frequency options**
  - 862-930 MHz
- **Connectors**
  - 2 USB type-C port
  - 2 Grove Connectors: GPS, and 400+ Grove Sensors
  - Antenna Female connector

### Features

- **Screen**: 3.95 Inch, Capacitive RGB Touch Screen.
- **2 Grove Connectors**: 400+ Grove-compatible GPIOs for flexible expansion options.
