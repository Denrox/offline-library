# Cardiospermum Halicacabum Flowering Top

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 2

**Also sold as:** Florasone, Florasone Cream

## Active ingredients

Contains 10% Cardiospermum halicacabum tincture

## Purpose

Temporarily relieves itch, rash, and skin irritation/inflammation.

## Uses

Temporarily relieves itch, rash, and skin irritation/inflammation.

## Warnings

For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes and broken skin.

### Do not use

Do not use if hypersensitive to any ingredients in this product. Breast-feeding woman should not apply to breast area.

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

Stop Use Stop use and ask a doctor if increased skin irritation, rash or other allergic reaction develops, condition worsens or does not improve within 7 days, symptoms clear up and occur again within a few days. These could be signs of a serious condition.

### Pregnancy or breast-feeding

Pregnancy or Breast-feeding If pregnant or breast-feeding, ask a healthcare professional before use.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children Keep out of reach of children.

## Directions

Dosage & Administration Directions Adults & children 6 years of age and older: apply to affected area up to 4 times daily; r ub in gently. Patch test recommended on sensitive skin.

## Inactive ingredients

Benzyl Alcohol Cetyl Alcohol Ethyl Alcohol Glycerin Glycol Stearate Isopropyl Myristate Mineral Oil Potassium Hydroxide Purified Water Stearyl Heptanoate
