# Borneol, Brucea Javanica Fruit, Camphor (natural) and Cnidium Monnieri Fruit

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 2

**Also sold as:** Yehoyod Wart Corn Remover, Fast-acting Wart Remover

## Active ingredients

Brucea javanica fruit 1% Cnidium monnieri fruit 0.5% Borneol 1.5% Camphor(natural) 0.8%

## Purpose

Wart remover

## Uses

For the removal of common warts and plantar warts.

## Warnings

For external use only.

### Do not use

on moles, birthmarks, unusual warts with hair growing from them, or genital warts. Do not apply to broken skin or healthy skin surrounding the wart.

### When using this product

Avoid contact with eyes, mouth, nostrils, and other mucous membranes. If contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water.

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

May cause irritation. Discontinue use if severe discomfort or sensitivity occurs.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out ofreach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

## Directions

Clean and dry the affected skin area. Apply a small amount to the wart using the dropper for broad coverage or the brush for precise application. Use 2 to 3 times daily.Allowto dry. Small tags (1-3 mm) typically shed within 7 days with over 90% success rate; medium tags (3-5 mm) gradually dry and fall off within 14 days, with nor ecurrence reported.

## Inactive ingredients

Lithospermum erythrorhizon whole Curcuma zedoaria root schisandra chinensis fruit Artemisia argyi whole Purified Water
