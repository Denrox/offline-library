## Amateur radio (ham) regions

Amateur radio allocations are chosen by ITU region rather than by country, and can only be selected in [licensed mode](User%20Configuration.md).

| ITU Region | Covers                                               | LoRa Regions                                 |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| 1          | Europe, Africa, the Middle East, and the former USSR | `ITU1_2M``ITU1_70CM`                   |
| 2          | The Americas                                         | `ITU2_2M``ITU2_125CM``ITU2_70CM` |
| 3          | Asia and the Pacific                                 | `ITU3_2M``ITU3_70CM`                   |

Individual administrations do not all allocate the full range each region covers, and power limits for licensed operators vary widely. Check your national band plan before transmitting.
