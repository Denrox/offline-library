# What is this radio antenna connector called?

*Tags: antenna, coaxial-cable, connectors · score 3*

## Question

I have this radio antenna, and need to replace the coaxial cable, but cannot figure out what this connection is called?

## Answer (score 2, by user10489)

This looks like an F connector, commonly used for TV antennas, and probably 75 ohms.

The male end typically uses the solid center conductor of RG6 or RG59 as its center pin.
