## Uses

How to Use: Apply evenly to the face as the final step of your skincare routine. Gently press into the skin until fully absorbed.

## Warnings

Cautions: 1. Discontinue use if the following symptoms occur. Consult a dermatologist if symptoms persist. 1) Redness, swelling, itching, or irritation during use. 2) Similar symptoms appear on applied areas after sun exposure. 2. Do not use on wounds, eczema, or inflamed skin. 3. Avoid contact with eyes. Rinse immediately if contact occurs.
