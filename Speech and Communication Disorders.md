# Speech and Communication Disorders

*Also called: Communication disorders*

Many disorders can affect our ability to speak and communicate. They range from saying sounds incorrectly to being completely unable to speak or understand speech. Causes include:

- [Hearing disorders and deafness](Hearing%20Disorders%20and%20Deafness.md)
- [Voice problems](Voice%20Disorders.md), such as dysphonia or those caused by [cleft lip or palate](Cleft%20Lip%20and%20Palate.md)
- Speech problems like [stuttering](Stuttering.md)
- [Developmental disabilities](Developmental%20Disabilities.md)
- [Learning disabilities](Learning%20Disabilities.md)
- [Autism spectrum disorder](Autism%20Spectrum%20Disorder.md)
- [Brain injury](Traumatic%20Brain%20Injury.md)
- [Stroke](Stroke.md)

Some speech and communication problems may be genetic. Often, no one knows the causes. By first grade, about 5% of [children](Speech%20and%20Language%20Problems%20in%20Children.md) have noticeable speech disorders. Speech and language therapy can help.
