# Benzethonium Chloride and Chlorhexidine Gluconate

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Hand Wipes

## Active ingredients

Benzethonium Chloride .......... 0.08 Chlorhexidine Digluconate ........ 0.10

## Purpose

Cleaning

## Uses

Cleans and refreshes hands and face without soap and water

## Warnings

For external use only. for external use on hands only. Stop use and sonsult a doctor if skin irritation develops. Store in a cool place. Keep out of reach of children. In case of accidental ingestion, seek professional assistance or contact a Poison Control Center immediately.

### Do not use

for external use on hands only. Stop use and sonsult a doctor if skin irritation develops. Store in a cool place.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children. In case of accidental ingestion, seek professional assistance or contact a Poison Control Center immediately.

## Directions

Self-dries in seconds. Leaves skin soft and smooth.

## Inactive ingredients

5-chloro-2-methyl-2H-isothiazol-3-one, Methylisothiazolinone, Polyaminopropyl Biguanide, Glycerol, Water
