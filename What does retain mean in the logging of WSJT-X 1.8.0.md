# What does retain mean in the logging of WSJT-X 1.8.0?

*Tags: ft8, wsjt-x · score 4*

## Question

If you have automatic prompt for logging on WSJT-X, at the end, you get this dialog:

What does the "Retain" checkboxes do when you check them?

## Accepted answer (score 6, by Phil Frost - W8II)

When those boxes are checked, the values entered will be retained as the defaults for the next QSO to be logged.

Unchecked, the fields will revert to blank.
