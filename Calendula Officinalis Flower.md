## Purpose

USES Promote rapid healing and helps prevent infection of wounds, skin ulcers and sores.

## Uses

Directions Wash and dry affected area, apply a thin layer of LMNOOP over affected area, gently massage until fully absorbed, repeat 2-3 times daily.

## Warnings

For external use only

### Do not use

do not get into eyes

### Ask a doctor before use if

Ask doctor if on deep or puncture wounds, animal bites or serious burns.

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

Stop use if condition worsens, symptoms last more than 7 days or clear up and occur again within a few days.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children if swallowed, call poison control or seek medical help.
