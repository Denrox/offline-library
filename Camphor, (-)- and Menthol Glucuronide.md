## Active ingredients

Menthol , Camphor

## Purpose

PRODUCT FEATURES: Effectively preventing and alleviating motion sickness for a smoother journey

## Uses

1.Tear open the package, take out the patch, peel off the white anti-adhesive layer, and stick the patchbehind the ear or the navel (please dry the sweat from the ear and the navel). 2.Use it 10 minutes before boarding a car, airplane or boat.*lf the symptoms are severe, you can use 2 patches on both ears or on one ear and navel

## Warnings

Pregnant women, breastfeeding mothers, children under the age of 4 should not use .Do not use if the skin has abnormalities, wounds, eczema, inflammation, allergies. Please keep it out of reach of children and pets. For external use only.
