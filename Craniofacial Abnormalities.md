# Craniofacial Abnormalities

Craniofacial is a medical term that relates to the bones of the skull and face. Craniofacial abnormalities are [birth defects](https://medlineplus.gov/birthdefects.html) of the face or head. Some, like [cleft lip and palate](https://medlineplus.gov/cleftlipandpalate.html), are among the most common of all birth defects. Others are very rare. Most of them affect how a person's face or head looks. These conditions may also affect other parts of the body.

Treatment depends on the type of problem. Plastic and reconstructive surgery may help the person's appearance.
