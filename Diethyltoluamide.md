# Diethyltoluamide

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Mosquito Bug Off

## Active ingredients

DIETHYLTOLUAMIDE

## Purpose

mosquito repellents that prevent access to mosquitoes.

## Uses

You can spray it on your skin or clothes.

## Warnings

do not use for the following people: infants under six months do not use excessive amounts or for long periods of time beyond the required do not use it in an enclosed area

### Keep out of reach of children

keep out of reach of the children

## Directions

for external use only

## Inactive ingredients

ET5HANOL, PURIFIED WATER, DIPROPYLENE GLYCOL, FRAGRANCE
