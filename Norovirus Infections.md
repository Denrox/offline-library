# Norovirus Infections

*Also called: Norwalk virus infections*

Noroviruses are a group of related viruses. Infection with these viruses causes an illness called [gastroenteritis](https://medlineplus.gov/gastroenteritis.html), an inflammation of the stomach and intestines. It can spread from person to person, or through contaminated food or water. You can also get it if you touch a contaminated surface. Norovirus can be serious, especially for young children and older adults.

The most common symptoms of norovirus infection are:

- Diarrhea
- Nausea and vomiting
- Stomach pain

Other symptoms may include fever, headache or body aches.

Treatment includes bed rest and lots of liquids to prevent [dehydration](https://medlineplus.gov/dehydration.html). There is no specific medicine to treat norovirus infections.

Proper hand washing and safe food preparation may help prevent infections.
