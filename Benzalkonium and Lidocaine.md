## Warnings

Section Warnings for external use only

### Do not use

OTC Do not use Do not use in or near the eyes, in large quantities or over large areas of the body, on raw surfaces or blistered areas

### Ask a doctor before use if

OTC-Ask Doctor Section Ask a doctor if you have deep puncture wounds, animal bites, serious burns

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

Stop Use and ask doctor if condition worsens or does not improve symptoms persist for more than 7 days or clear up and occur again within a few days
