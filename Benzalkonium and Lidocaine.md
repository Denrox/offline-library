# Benzalkonium and Lidocaine

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Drug class:** Amide Local Anesthetic, Antiarrhythmic  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Walgreens Burn Gel Packettes

## Active ingredients

OTC-Active Ingredient Section Active Ingredients Benzalkonium Chloride 0.13% - First Aid Antiseptic Lidocain HCl 4% - Topical Analgesic

## Purpose

First aid antiseptic Topical analgesic

## Uses

& Usage Section Uses For temporary relief of pain and itching associated with sunburn, minor burns, minor skin irritation, minor cuts, minor scrapes, insect bites

## Warnings

Section Warnings for external use only

### Do not use

OTC Do not use Do not use in or near the eyes, in large quantities or over large areas of the body, on raw surfaces or blistered areas

### Ask a doctor before use if

OTC-Ask Doctor Section Ask a doctor if you have deep puncture wounds, animal bites, serious burns

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

Stop Use and ask doctor if condition worsens or does not improve symptoms persist for more than 7 days or clear up and occur again within a few days

### Pregnancy or breast-feeding

OTC Pregnancy or Breastfeeding Section If pregnant or breastfeeding ask a health professional before use.

### Keep out of reach of children

OTC Keep Out of Reach of Children Section Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help orn contact a poison control center right away

## Directions

Instrutions for use section Directions Adults and children 2 years and older. clean the burn area with mild soap and water, apply a small amount of gel to the affected area not more than 3 times daily. May be covered with a sterile bandage (let dry first). Children under 2 years of age consult a doctor.
