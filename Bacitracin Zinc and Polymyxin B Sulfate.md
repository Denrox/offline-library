## Uses

First aid to help prevent the risk of infection in minor cuts, scrapes, and burns

## Warnings

For external use only. Do not use if you are allergic to any of the ingredients in the eyes over large areas of the body Ask a doctor before use if you have deep or puncture wounds animal bites serious burns Stop use and ask a doctor if you need to use longer than 1 week condition persists or gets worse rash or other allergic reaction develops If pregnant or breast-feeding, ask a health professional before use. Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away: 800-222-1222.

## Directions

clean the affected area apply a small amount of this product (an amount equal to the surface area of the tip of a finger) on the area up to 3 times daily may be covered with a sterile bandage
