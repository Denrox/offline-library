## Active ingredients

(s) Sodium fluoride 0.243%W/W (equivalent to 1100 ppm f.)

## Purpose

Anticavity

## Uses

Helps protect against cavities.

## Warnings

As with all fluoride toothpaste, keep out of reach of children under 6 years old. If you accidentally swallow more than used for brushing, get medical help or call a Poison Control Center right away.

### Do not use

Do not use in eye area

### When using this product

Do not swallow

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

If you have an allergic reaction after using this product, please stop using it immediately and see a doctor.

### Keep out of reach of children

Please keep it out of the reach of children.

## Directions

Adults and children 2 years and over: Brush teeth thoroughly after each meal or at least twice a day, or as directed by a dentist or doctor. Children under 6 years of age: Use a pea-sized amount and supervise child's brushing and rinsing (to minimize swallowing). For children under 2 years of age: Ask a dentist or doctor.

## Other information

Store in a cool and shady place
