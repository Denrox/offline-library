# Goldenseal

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** oral  
**Labels on file:** 3

**Also sold as:** Hydrastis Canadensis

## Active ingredients

HYDRASTIS

## Purpose

USES To relieve the symptoms of sinuses.

## Uses

Indications: HYDRASTIS Sinuses

## Warnings

STOP USE AND ASK DOCTOR If symptoms persist/worsen or if pregnant/nursing, stop use and consult your practitioner.

### Keep out of reach of children

KEEP OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN Keep this and all medicines out of reach of children.

## Directions

Adults: Dissolve 3 to 5 under the tongue three times a day or as directed by Lic. Practitioner. Take at greater intervals as condition subsides. Children: Dissolve 3 to 5 under the tongue three times a day or as directed by Lic. Practitioner. Take at greater intervals as condition subsides.

## Inactive ingredients

Sucrose/Lactose
