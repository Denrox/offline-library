## Active ingredients

Dimethicone 1% Zinc oxide 10% Purpose Skin protectant

## Purpose

Skin protectant

## Uses

helps prevent diaper rash heals dry, flaky and chafed skin soothes and protects minor cuts and burns

## Warnings

For external use only. Do not use over large areas of the body not for extensive burns or serious cuts, consult a physician When using this product avoid contact with eyes Ask a doctor before use serious burns deep wounds Stop use and ask a doctor if symptoms last for more than 7 days redness, irritation, swelling or pain persists or increases or if infection develops. symptoms clear up and then occur again within a few days Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away (1-800-222-1222).
