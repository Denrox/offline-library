# Does a UHF/VHF handheld transceiver kit exist?

*Tags: vhf, uhf, ht, transceiver · score 4*

## Question

I am a licensed ham looking for a UHF/VHF handheld transceiver kit. Unfortunately so far I was only able to find broken links, i.e. Ten-Tec used to produce exactly such a thing but they just don't anymore. I'm looking for a kit, not a finished product. My search has not been successful so far.

Are there **any** such kits in current production?
