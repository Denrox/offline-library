# RAKwireless Devices (rak wireless)

RAKwireless offers a variety of Meshtastic-compatible hardware, including modular starter kits and ready-to-use prebuilt devices. The RAK Meshtastic Collection showcases both options, making it easy to find the right solution for your needs. If you're assembling a Meshtastic device using RAK hardware, the RAK Meshtastic Designer helps you select the right modules to match your use case.

## RAK WisMesh

RAK WisMesh is a series of ready-to-use Meshtastic devices designed for seamless deployment. Unlike the WisBlock modular system, which allows users to build and customize their own devices, WisMesh devices provide pre-built, purpose-driven solutions that eliminate the need for assembly or additional configuration.
