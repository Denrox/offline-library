# Ethylhexyl Methoxycinnamate and Zinc Oxide

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Murasaki Beauty Silicence Scar Gel Spf 30

## Active ingredients

Zinc Oxide 6% Ethylhexyl Methoxycinnamate 1%

## Uses

Helps prevent sunburn. ​Protects scars from UV exposure, which may help reduce discoloration and further skin damage.

## Warnings

For external use only. Do not use on damaged or broken skin or open wounds. Keep out of eyes. If contact occurs, rinse eyes thoroughly with water. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away. Stop use and ask a doctor if rash or irritation occurs.

## Directions

FOR SUNSCREEN USE: Apply liberally 15 minutes before sun exposure. Reapply at least every 2 hours. Use a water-resistant sunscreen if swimming or sweating. SUN PROTECTION MEASURES: Regularly use a sunscreen with a Broad Spectrum SPF value of 15 or higher and other sun protection measures.

## Inactive ingredients

Deionized Water, Polydimethylsiloxane, Hyaluronic Acid, Squalane, Carnosine, Emulsifier
