# Ascorbic Acid, Folic Acid, Methylcobalamin, Niacinamide, Pantothenic Acid, Pyridoxine, Riboflavin and Thiamine

> **Note:** Official U.S. prescribing information, written for health professionals; very long sections are shortened. Take prescription medicines only as prescribed, and never start, stop or change a dose without asking your doctor or pharmacist. Not medical advice.

**Route:** oral  
**Ingredient forms:** Thiamine Hydrochloride, Pyridoxine Hydrochloride  
**Drug class:** Vitamin C  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Brand and product names:** Lumavex

## Uses

Lumavex is a prescription multivitamin/multi-mineral dietary supplement formulated for the clinical dietary management of a subuoptimal nutritional status in patients where advanced folate and maintenance of good health is needed.

## Dosage

Usual adult dosage is one to two capsule daily or as directed by a licensed medical practitioner.

## Contraindications

Lumavex is contraindicated in patients with a known hypersensitivity to any of the ingredients.

## Warnings

If you are pregnant or nursing, ask a health care professional before using this product. Keep this product out of the reach of children.

## Precautions

Folic acid alone is improper therapy in the treatment of pernicious anemia and other megaloblastic anemias where Vitamin B 12 deficient. Folic acid in doses above 1.0 mg daily may obscure pernicious anemia in that hematologic remission can occur while neurological manifestations progress.

## Side effects

Call your doctor about side effects. You may report side effects to the FDA at 1-800-FDA-1088.
