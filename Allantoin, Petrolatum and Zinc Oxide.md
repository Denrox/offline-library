## Uses

helps treat and prevent diaper rash protects chafed skin due to diaper rash and helps seal out wetness

## Warnings

For external use only. When using this product do not get into eyes Stop use and ask a doctor if condition worsens symptoms last for more than 7 days or clear up and occur again within a few days Keep out of the reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

## Directions

change wet and soiled diapers promptly cleanse the diaper area allow to dry apply ointment liberally as often as necessary, with each diaper change, especially at bedtime or anytime when exposure to wet diapers may be prolonged

## Other information

store at room temperature

## Inactive ingredients
