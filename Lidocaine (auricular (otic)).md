# Lidocaine (auricular (otic))

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** auricular (otic)  
**Drug class:** Amide Local Anesthetic, Antiarrhythmic  
**Labels on file:** 2

**Also sold as:** Earbalance Ear Pain Drops

## Active ingredients

Lidocaine HCl 4% Back

## Purpose

Local anesthetic

## Uses

Temporarily relieves ear discomfort and minor pain associated with swimmer's ear, ear canal irritation, and post-irrigation soreness.

## Warnings

For use in the ear canal only.

### Do not use

if the eardrum is perforated or ruptured if you have ear tubes if you are allergic to lidocaine or any ingredient in this product

### Ask a doctor before use if

you have ear drainage, discharge, bleeding, fever, sudden hearing loss, dizziness, or ringing in the ears

### When using this product

use only in the ear canal avoid contact with eyes do not swallow temporary numbness may occur

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

pain worsens or lasts longer than 48 hours irritation, redness, or allergic reaction occurs symptoms occur with fever or discharge

### Pregnancy or breast-feeding

If pregnant or breast-feeding ask a health professional before use

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children If swallowed, contact a Poison Control Center right away at 1-800-222-1222.

## Directions

Adults and children 6 years and older: place 3 to 5 drops into affected ear up to 4 times daily. Keep head tilted for 2 to 3 minutes after application. Children under 6 years: ask a doctor.

## Inactive ingredients

Alcohol USP, Organic Apple Cider Vinegar, Echinacea (Echinacea purpurea) Extract, Goldenseal (Hydrastis canadensis) Root Extract.
