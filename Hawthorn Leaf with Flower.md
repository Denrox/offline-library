## Active ingredients

Malt(20%), Hawthorn(20%)

## Purpose

digestive enhancement, relief of stomach pain, improvement of digestive function

## Uses

Take 1 pouch (3g) three time a day after a meal

## Warnings

If you have an unusual or allergic constitution, check the ingredients before consuming.

keep out of reach of the children

for oral use only
