## Purpose

Moisturizer

## Uses

Moisturizing dry nasal passages and relieves nasal dryness associated with colds, allergies, sinusitis or dry air.

## Warnings

Not for Injection Do not use after expiration date Discontinue if you experience any adverse reactions and call your physician Only use sealed vials Do not use if vial is broken

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away at 1-800-222-1222

## Directions

Adults and children: Administer into nasal passageway as often as needed or as directed by a physician. For infants, apply using drops. Follow the instructions provided with the delivery device for proper administration. To help prevent the spread of infection, do not share the dispenser or delivery device with others.
