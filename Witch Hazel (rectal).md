# Witch Hazel (rectal)

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** rectal  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Hemorrhoidal Hygiene Pads

## Active ingredients

Drug Facts Witch Hazel 50% Purpose: Hemorrhoidal Astringent

## Purpose

Uses For temporary relief of local discomfort, burning and irritation associated with Hemorrhoids

## Uses

Directions: When practical, clean the affected area with mild soap and warm water, and rinse thoroughly

## Warnings

For External Use Only Avoid contact with the eyes Consult a doctor If condition worsens or does not improve within 7 days In case of bleeding Before exceeding the recommended dosage DO NOT put this product into the rectum using fingers or any mechanical device.

### Keep out of reach of children

KEEP OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN If swallowed, get medical attention or contact the Poison Control Center immediately.

## Directions

Gently dry by patting or blotting with toilet tissue or a soft cloth before applying Apply externally to the affected area up to 6 times daily or after each bowel movement After application, discard pad Children under 12 years of age: consult a doctor

## Other information

Store at room temperature 15°-30°C (59°-86°F) If adverse effects call 1-877-225-6999

## Inactive ingredients

Other Ingredients Alcohol, Citric Acid, Deionized Water, DMDM Hydantoin,Glycerin, Sodium Citrate.
