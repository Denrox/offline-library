# Cholecalciferol and Sodium Fluoride

> **Note:** Official U.S. prescribing information, written for health professionals; very long sections are shortened. Take prescription medicines only as prescribed, and never start, stop or change a dose without asking your doctor or pharmacist. Not medical advice.

**Route:** oral  
**Drug class:** Vitamin D  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Brand and product names:** Floriva

## Uses

How to Use Fill dropper to appropriate line and dispense with a single squeeze of the dropper bulb. The full dose will be given. It is normal for a small amount to remain in the tip of the dropper. Do not accept if tamper evident box seal is broken or missing. The manufacturer of this product requires that it be dispensed only under the order of a physician or licensed medical practitioner.

## Dosage

Directions Use as directed by a physician

## Warnings

KEEP THIS PRODUCT OUT OF THE REACH OF CHILDREN. IF OVERDOSAGE IS SUSPECTED, SEEK PROFESSIONAL ASSISTANCE OR CONTACT A POISON CONTROL CENTER IMMEDIATELY 1-800-222-1222. SHAKE WELL BEFORE USE
