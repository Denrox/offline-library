## Active ingredients

Methyl Salicylate 25%

## Purpose

Topical Analgesic

## Uses

Temporary relief of minor aches and pains associated with: simple backache arthritis bruises strains sprains

## Warnings

For external use only Allergy Alert: If prone to allergic reaction from aspirin or other salicylates, consult a doctor before use. Do not use on wounds or damaged skin with a heating pad When using this product use only as directed avoid contact with eyes and mucous membranes do not bandage tightly Stop use and ask a doctor if condition worsens redness or severe skin irritation occurs symptoms persist for more than 7 days symptoms clear up and occur again within a few days If pregnant or breast-feeding ask a healthcare professional before use. Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
