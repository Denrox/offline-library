## Active ingredients

MINERAL OIL、LAMINARIA DIGITATA EXTRACT、HELIANTHUS ANNUUS (SUNFLOWER) SEED EXTRACT

## Purpose

1. It can effectively protect your scalp and hair from UV damage, prevent hair color fading and hair dryness. 2. It can maintain the balance between scalp and hair oil, and say goodbye to the sticky feeling in summer. 3. It can provide sufficient moisture for your hair and scalp and protect your scalp and hair from damage. 4. It uses mild ingredients and can be completely absorbed, without traces and will not damage your hairstyle.

## Uses

1. Clean your hair and scalp completely and keep them dry. 2. Shake the spray bottle evenly to ensure that the ingredients inside are fully mixed, and spray it evenly on the scalp and hair. 3. Gently massage your scalp with your fingers to ensure that the product is fully penetrated into the scalp and hair.
