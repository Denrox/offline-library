# Camphor (synthetic), Levomenthol and Methyl Salicylate (transdermal)

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** transdermal  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Zenol Double Action

## Active ingredients

Methyl salicylate L-Menthol dl-Camphor

## Purpose

For temporary relief of minor aches & pains of muscles & joints Associated with: Arthritis, Simple backache, Strains, Bruises, Sprains

## Uses

Adults and children 12 years of age and over: Clean and dry affected area Remove patch from film Apply to affected area not more than 3 to 4 times daily Remove patch from the skin after at most 8-hour application Children under 12 years of age: Consult a doctor

## Warnings

For external use only Use only as directed Avoid contact with eyes, mucous membranes or rashes Do not bandage tightly

### Keep out of reach of children

keep out of reach of the children

## Directions

for external use only

## Inactive ingredients

Kaolin Zinc oxide Titanium oxide Gelatin Glycerin Liquid paraffin Polybutene Sodium polyacrylate Rubber Latex Purified Water
