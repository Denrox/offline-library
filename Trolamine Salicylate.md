## Active ingredients

Trolamine salicylate 10%

## Purpose

Topical analgesic

## Uses

temporarily relieves minor pain associated with:

- arthritis
- simple backache
- muscle strains
- sprains
- bruises
- cramps

## Warnings

For external use only Allergy alert: If prone to allergic reaction from aspirin or salicylates, consult a doctor before use. When using this product

- use only as directed
- do not bandage tightly or use with a heating pad
- avoid contact with eyes or mucous membranes
- do not apply to wounds or damaged skin. Stop use and ask a doctor if
- condition worsens
- symptoms persist for more than 7 days or clear up and occur again within a few days
- redness is present
- irritation develops. If pregnant or breast-feeding, ask a health professional before use. Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
