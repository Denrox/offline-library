# Alpha-tocopherol and Hyaluronic Acid

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Ingredient forms:** Alpha-tocopherol Acetate  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Eyeone Cleaner

## Active ingredients

Hyaluronic acid, Tocopheryl Acetate

## Purpose

It cleans the area of eyebrow oil and glands and moisturizes the dryness around the eyes

## Uses

it whenever you need to clean your eyelids

## Warnings

1. Consult a specialist if you have abnormal symptoms or side effects such as red spots, swelling, or itching due to direct sunlight during or after using this product. 2. Please refrain from using the wound area 3. Precautions for Storage and Handling A. Please keep it out of the reach of children. B. Please store it in a cool place. 4. Wash it immediately when it gets into your eyes.

### Keep out of reach of children

keep it out of the reach of children.

## Directions

It is generally used twice a day, in the morning and evening. Or use it whenever you need to clean your eyelids. - Wash your hands thoroughly before use. - Remove the pad after opening the packet. - Close your eyes and wipe the eyelids and the base of your eyelashes with cloth. - Once used, throw away cloth without fail.

## Inactive ingredients

WATER,1,2-Hexanediol, Sodium Benzoate, PEG-8 CAPRYLIC/CAPRIC GLYCERIDES, PEG-6 CAPRYLIC/CAPRIC GLYCERIDES, POLOXAMER 184, Caprylyl Glycol, POLYSORBATE 20
