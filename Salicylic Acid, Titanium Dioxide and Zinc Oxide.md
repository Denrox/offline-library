## Purpose

- Helps prevent sunburn
- If used as directed with other sun protection measures (see Directions), decreases the risk of skin cancer and early skin aging caused by the sun

## Uses

- apply liberally 15 minutes before sun exposure
- reapply: – after 80 minutes of swimming or sweating – immediately after towel drying – at least every 2 hours
- children under 6 months: Ask a doctor

## Warnings

For external use only Do not use

- on damaged or broken skin When using this product
- keep out of eyes. Rinse with water to remove. Stop use and ask a doctor if
- rash occurs Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
