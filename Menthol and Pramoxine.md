## Active ingredients

Menthol 0.5% Pramoxine Hydrochloride 1%

## Purpose

External analgesic

## Uses

for the temporary relief of pain and itching associated with minor burns, sunburn, minor cuts, scrapes, insect bites, minor skin irritations and rashes due to poison ivy, poison oak, or poison sumac

## Warnings

For external use only. Do not use in the eyes or nose over large areas of the body for prolonged periods When using this product avoid contact with the eyes. if condition worsens, or if symptoms persist for more than 7 days or clear up and occur again within a few days, discontinue use of this product and consult a doctor. Stop use and ask a doctor if redness, irritation, swelling, or pain persists or increases. Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
