## Active ingredients

DIMETHICONE, UNSPECIFIED 98% TOCOPHEROL 1% ASIATICOSIDE 0.5% ALLANTOIN 0.2%

## Purpose

Advanced silicone care for visible scar improvement.

## Uses

： Surgical Scars Burn Scars Hypertrophic Scars Hyperpigmented Scars C-Section Scars

## Warnings

For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. Do not apply to open wounds or infected skin. Discontinue use if irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children.

### Keep out of reach of children

Avoid contact with eyes.Keep out of reach of children.

## Directions

1.Clean and dry the affected area. 2.Apply a thin, even layer over the scar. 3.Allow 3-5 minutes for the gel to dry completely. 4.Once dry,makeup,sunscreen,and other skincare products may be applied as usual. 5.Use twice daily for best resuits.
