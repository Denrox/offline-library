## Warnings

For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. Discontinue use if irritation occurs. Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

### When using this product

Apply to clean, dry skin 15 minutes before sun exposure. Reapply every 2 hours or after swimming, sweating, or towel drying.
