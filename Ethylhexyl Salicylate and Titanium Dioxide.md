# Ethylhexyl Salicylate and Titanium Dioxide

**Route:** cutaneous  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Sunscreen

## Active ingredients

TITANIUM DIOXIDE 2.85% ETHYLHEXYL SALICYLATE 3%

## Purpose

Sun protection

## Uses

Helps prevent sunburn If used as directed with other sun protection measures(see Directions),decreases the risk of skin cancer and early skin aging caused by the sun.

## Warnings

For external use only. Keep out of reach of children. Avoid contactwith eyes.Discontinue use if signs of irritation or rash occur, Store in a cooand dry place.

### Keep out of reach of children

Do not use on broken skin; avoid contact with eyes.lf product gets into eyes, rinseimmediately with plentyof water.Discontinue use if discomfort occurs

## Directions

Shake the contents thoroughly, apply appropriate amount to the palm, evenly apply to the face, neck, and body skin, and evenly pat around until absorbed
