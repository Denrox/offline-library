# Citric Acid (hemodialysis)

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** hemodialysis  
**Ingredient forms:** Citric Acid Monohydrate  
**Labels on file:** 2

**Also sold as:** Citracid-50, Aquacetic-30

## Active ingredients

Citric Acid

## Purpose

For use with 3-pump hemodialysis machines only.

## Uses

For use with 3-pump hemodialysisi machines only, using purified water (StandardUNE-EN ISO 23500-3:2019).

## Warnings

Avoid contact with skin an eyes. Irritation hazard if spilled or splashed.

### Do not use

If the warranty seal is damaged or broken and do not allow debris to fall into the concentrate.

### When using this product

Take appropriate security measures (protective clothing, glasses, masks). Wash splashes on skin or clothing with plenty of water.

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

If do not use the entire contents, discard the excess.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

## Directions

By diluting one part of this acid concentrate with one part of purified water.

## Other information

Store at room temperature. Keep container tightly closed when not in use.
