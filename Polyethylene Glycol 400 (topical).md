# Polyethylene Glycol 400 (topical)

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Dr.harry Eyes Protection

## Active ingredients

Polyethylene glycol 400 1%

## Purpose

Lubricant

## Uses

Refreshes eye contour,delivers fresh sensation, hydrates and maintains eye skin moisture for instant refreshing comfort.

## Warnings

For external use only.

### Do not use

if solution changes color or becomes cloudy

### Ask a doctor before use if

Ask Doctor Discontinue use if irritation appears. Seek medical help for eye stinging, itching or blurred vision.

### When using this product

When Using If pregnant or breast‑feeding,ask a health professional before use. Do not swallow. Avoid eye contact; rinse well with water if contact occurs.

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

Stop Use Discontinue use if irritation appears. Seek medical help for eye stinging, itching or blurred vision.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep Out Of Reach Of Children Keep away from children & pets.

## Directions

Spray 1‑2 times onto closed eyelids from about 10cm away,up to 4 times daily.

## Other information

Use within 6 months of opening. Do not freeze. Do not use if bottle is damaged. Store cool and dry, avoid direct sunlight.

## Inactive ingredients

Calendula Officinalis Flower Extract,Lutein,Hydrolyzed Sodium Hyaluronate, Purified Water,Vitamin B5,Vitamin A Palmitate, Vitamin E,Glycerin,Butylene Glycol,Ethylhexylglycerin.
