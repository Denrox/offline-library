## Warnings

For use in the ear only Ask a doctor before use if you have ear drainage or discharge ear pain irritation, or rash in the ear dizziness an injury or perforation (hole) of the ear drum recently had ear surgery When using this product avoid contact with the eyes Stop use and ask a doctor if you need to use for more than 4 days excessive earwax remains after use of this product Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
