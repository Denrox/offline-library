**Also sold as:** Medclot Hemostatic Dressing 10 Cm X 10 Cm, Medclot Hemostatic Dressing 7.5 Cm X 7.5 Cm, Medclot Hemostatic Dressing 7.5 Cm X 150 Cm, Medclot Hemostatic Dressing 7.5 Cm X 370 Cm

## Active ingredients

CarboxymethylCellulose

## Purpose

Emergecy bleeding control. For use by trained emergency responders.

## Uses

1. Open package and remove MED-CLOT. 2. Pack MED-CLOT into the wound and use it to apply pressure directly over bleeding source. More than one MED-CLOT may be required. 3. Continue to apply pressure untill bleeding is under control. 4. Wrap and tie bandage to maintain pressure. Seek medical care immediately. Show PRODUCT REMOVAL directions on package to medical personnel.

## Warnings

For external use only. Sterility not guaranteed if package is damaged. Avoid contact with eyes. Reuse will cause risk of infection and loss of efficacy.

### Do not use

if package is damaged.

### When using this product

Reuse will cause risk of infection and loss of efficacy.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children. Avoid contact with eyes.

## Directions

More than one MED-CLOT may be required.

## Other information

Keep at room temperature.

## Inactive ingredients

Purified water (standard ISO 13959:2014)
