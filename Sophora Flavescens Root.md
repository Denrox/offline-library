## Uses

1.For the first use, soak the sick nail in warm water. 2 Thin the irregular nail with a file. 3 Drop an appropriate amount of this product and slowly penetrate into the diseased nail 4 One drop in the morning and one drop in the evening. Use it continuously until it heals.
