## Active ingredients

BEE VENOM、ALOE BARBADENSIS LEAF EXTRACT

## Purpose

1. 1. Deeply nourish the nail bed and nails, help soften the nails, gradually fade nail discoloration, and reduce the formation of onychomycosis from the source. 2. Adding bee venom can promote skin metabolism, enhance local circulation, help improve the nutrient supply status of the nail bed, and thus promote the growth of healthy nails. 3. Long-term use can significantly improve nail health and restore its luster.

## Uses

1.Keep nails and surrounding skin dry. 2. Take an appropriate amount of solution and apply it on the nails and surrounding skin and wait for absorption. It is recommended to apply it once a day in the morning and evening.

## Warnings

Please keep out of reach of children. Do not swallow.Please clean your hands before use to ensure the best results from the product. Discontinue use if signs of irritation or rash occur. Store in a cool and dry place.
