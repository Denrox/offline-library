# Magnesium Sulfate Heptahydrate, Monobasic, Potassium Chloride, Sodium Chloride and Sodium Phosphate, Dibasic, Heptahydrate

> **Note:** Official U.S. prescribing information, written for health professionals; very long sections are shortened. Take prescription medicines only as prescribed, and never start, stop or change a dose without asking your doctor or pharmacist. Not medical advice.

**Route:** irrigation  
**Ingredient forms:** Monobasic Potassium Phosphate  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Brand and product names:** Tis-u-sol

## Uses

Isotonic solution for irrigation only. Not for injection.

## Dosage

As directed by a physician. Cautions: Warm in oven to not more than 50°C for a maximum of 60 days. Discard after 60 days of warming. Do not use unless solution is clear and seal is intact. Discard unused portion. Rx only.
