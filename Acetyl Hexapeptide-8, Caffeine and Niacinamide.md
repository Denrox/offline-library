# Acetyl Hexapeptide-8, Caffeine and Niacinamide

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Drug class:** Central Nervous System Stimulant, Methylxanthine  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Anti-wrinkle Eye Cream

## Purpose

Temporarily reduces under-eye puffiness Helps improve the appearance of dark circles Minimizes the look of fine lines and wrinkles

## Uses

Temporarily reduces under-eye puffiness Helps improve the appearance of dark circles Minimizes the look of fine lines and wrinkles

## Warnings

Do not take orally. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

### Do not use

If you are allergic to any of the ingredients. On broken or irritated skin. When using this product Discontinue use if redness,irritation, or swelling occurs. Keep away from the eye contour to prevent stinging.

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

Stop use and consult a doctor if irritation persists or worsens.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children,The above warning signs have been listed in the labels

## Directions

Apply a small amount to the under-eye area using gentle patting motions. Use once or twice daily,preferably in the morning and/or evening. Avoid direct contact with eyes.

## Inactive ingredients

Aqua,Glycerin,Squalane,Sodium Hyaluronate,Cetearyl Alcohol, Phenoxyethanol, Ethylhexylglycerin
