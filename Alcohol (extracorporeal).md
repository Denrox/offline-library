# Alcohol (extracorporeal)

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** extracorporeal  
**Labels on file:** 10

**Also sold as:** Hand Sanitizer, 75% Alcohol Disinfectant

## Active ingredients

(s) Ethyl Alcohol 75% v/v

## Purpose

Antiseptic

## Uses

Health care personnel hand rub to help reduce bacteria that potentially can cause disease.

## Warnings

For external use only. Flammable. Keep away from heat or flame.

### Do not use

in children less than 2 months of age on open skin wounds

### When using this product

keep out of eyes, ears, and mouth. In case of contact with eyes, rinse eyes thoroughly with water.

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

irritation or rash occurs.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

## Directions

Place enough product on hands to cover all surfaces. Rub hands together until dry. Supervise children under 6 years of age when using this product to avoid swallowing.

## Other information

Store between 15-30℃(59-86℉) Avoid freezing and excessive heat above 40℃(104℉)

## Inactive ingredients

Water(Aqua, Eau).
