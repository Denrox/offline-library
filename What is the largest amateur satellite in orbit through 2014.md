# What is the largest amateur satellite in orbit through 2014?

*Tags: satellites · score 3*

## Question

What is the largest, and what were the dimensions of the largest amateur radio designated satellite in Earth orbit as of, or up until, 2014 other than the International space station.

## Accepted answer (score 1, by PearsonArtPhoto)

According to my sources, the 3 biggest Amateur Radio satellites (By Radar Cross Section) are:

- International Space Station
- Oceansat 1- Had a small Amateur Radio payload
- AO-40 Dedicated Amateur Radio satellite.
