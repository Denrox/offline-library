# Lidocaine (cutaneous, topical, transdermal)

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** cutaneous, topical, transdermal  
**Drug class:** Amide Local Anesthetic, Antiarrhythmic  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Sinsinpas Lidocaine Pain Relieving

## Active ingredients

Lidocaine 4%

## Purpose

Topical Anesthetic

## Uses

For the temporarily relief of minor pain

## Warnings

For external use only Do not use more than 1 patch at a time on broken, irritated, or swollen skin on puncture wounds for more than one week without consulting a doctor When using this product use only as directed. Read and follow all direction and warning on this label Avoid contact with the eyes or mucous membranes Do not apply to broken, damaged, or irritated skin Do not bandage tightly or apply local heat (such as heating pads) to the area of use Rare cases of serious burns have been reported with products of this type Do not use with other topical analgesics Keep children and pets away from used or disposed patch. Used patches still contain the drug product that can produce serious adverse effects if a child or pet chews or ingests this product.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children or pets Keep out of reach of children or pets. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center (1-800-222-1222)

## Directions

Adults and children 12 years of age and over: clean and dry affected area remove film from patch by gently pulling apart from corner carefully apply to afftcted area once patch is positioned, press firmly to adhere to skin use only one patch at a time for up the 12 hours Children under 12 years of age: consult a professional
