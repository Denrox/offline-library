# Ammonium Chloride

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Aviva Sports Smelling Salts For Athletes

## Active ingredients

(s) Ammonium Chloride 50%

## Purpose

Inhalation

## Uses

For the temporary stimulation ofthe senses to restore alertness.

## Warnings

For inhalation use only. Do not ingest or apply to skin. Avoid direct contact with eyes. If contact occurs, rinse throughly with water for 15 minutes. Use only as directed. Misuse may be harmful. Use in a well-ventelated ares. Dó not use in enclosed spaces. Possible side effects include nasal irritation, coughing or shortness of breath.Ifsymptoms persist, discontinue use and consult a doctor.

### Do not use

If you have respiratory conditions such as asthma or emphysema. If you have heart disease or high blood pressure If you are allergic to ammonia or strong odors.

### When using this product

Avoid contact with eyes, ears, and mouth. If product gets into eyes, rinse thoroughly with water for 15 minutes.

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

irritation or rash occurs. These may be signs of a series condition.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a poison control center immediately at 1-800-222-1222.

## Directions

Add a capful of water (only for first use) Close and shake well. Hold bottle 4-6 inches (10-15 cm) from nostrils. Inhale gently. Do not place directly inside nostrils. Do not inhale for more than 2 seconds per use. Do not use more than once every 60 minutes. Do not use more than 10 time per day. Use only as needed. If symptoms persist, consult a healthcare professional.
