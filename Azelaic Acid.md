# Azelaic Acid

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 4

**Also sold as:** 14% Azelaic Acid Acne, Bloomellee 12%gel Azelaic Acid Acne, Bloomellee 14%gel Azelaic Acid Cream

## Active ingredients

Azelaic Acid 10%

## Purpose

Acne Treatment

## Uses

For the treatment of acne.

## Warnings

For external use only.

### Do not use

on broken skin; if allergic to any ingredients.

### Ask a doctor before use if

pregnant or nursing, or have any underlying medical condition.

### When using this product

avoid contact with eyes. If contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water.

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

significant facial swelling, large areas of red rash, intense itching or burning sensation.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children: if swallowed, get medical help or contact Poison Control Center right away.

## Directions

Cleanse face to remove dirt and impurities. Wash face and gently pat dry. Dispense cream onto hands and apply directly to acne-prone areas. Use twice daily (morning and evening).

Water, Butylene Glycol, Methylpropandiol, Dimethicone, Cetearyl Glucoside, Dipropylene Glycol, Sorbitan Olivate, Polyacrylate-6, Squalane, Cetyl Alcohol, Hydroxypropyl Cyclodextrin, 1,2-Hexanediol, 4-Hydroxyacetophenone, Hydroxyethylpiperazineethan Sulfate, Allantoin, Cyclodextrin, Caprylyl Glycine, Xanthan Gum, O-Cymene-5-ol, Madecassoside, Centella Asiatica Extract, Lavandula Angustifolia Oil, Centella Asiatica, Saurus Chinensis Leaf/Root Extract, Taraxacum Officinale Leaf Extract, Stephania Tetrandrae Extract.
