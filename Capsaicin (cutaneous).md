# Capsaicin (cutaneous)

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** cutaneous  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Neuronesic Pain Relieving Cream

## Active ingredients

DRUG FACTS Active Ingredients Capsaicin ............ 0.1%

## Purpose

............... Topical Analgesic

## Uses

- For temporary relief from minor aches and pains of sore muscles and joints associated with arthritis pain, simple backache, strains and sprains
- Provides warming pain relief

## Warnings

- for external use only Do not use:

- on wounds or damaged skin with a heating pad
- if allergic to capsicum or chili peppers When using this produc :
- test on a small area of skin before first use to check for skin sensitivity
- avoid contact with eyes or mucous membranes
- do not bandage tightly
- do not expose area to direct heat or direct sunlight
- a slight burning sensation may occur upon application and generally disappears within several days Stop use and ask a physician if :
- condition worsens
- redness is present
- symptoms persist for more than 7 days or clear up and occur again within a few days
- excessive skin irritation develops

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a poison control center (1-800-222-2222) immediately

## Directions

- Adults and children 18 years of age and older: Apply to affected area no more than 3 to 4 times daily. Wash hands with soap and water after each use.
- Children under 18 years of age: do not use, consult a physician.
