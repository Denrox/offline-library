**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** V-fresh Restore Gel

## Active ingredients

SOPHORA ALOPECUROIDES L 20%

## Purpose

Botanical Care

## Uses

Helps moisturize and maintain vaginal dryness Helps soothe and provide long-lasting comfort Supports intimate microbiome balance Helps maintain a healthy vaginal environment

## Warnings

For external and vaginal use only.

### Do not use

If you have an allergy to any of the ingredients If the seal on the tube is broken or missing

### Ask a doctor before use if

Vaginal itching, irritation, or abnormal discharge Vaginal bleeding Pain, fever, or chills

### When using this product

Use only as directed Do not use tampons, douches, spermicides or other vaginal products at the same time

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

Symptoms persist for more than 7 days Irritation, discomfort or pain occurs

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children. In case of overdose,get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

## Directions

Remove one tube from the package. Gently insert one tube into the vagina at bedtime. Use once daily or as recommended by a health professional. Discard the tube after use.

## Inactive ingredients

Cordyceps cranberry extract Crocus sativus Extract Purified water hyaluronic acid
