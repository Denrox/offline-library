# Camphor (natural) and Methyl Salicylate

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 2

**Also sold as:** Muscle Therapy, Elp Essential Manteca De Ubre

## Active ingredients

Organic Methyl Salicylate 10% Organic Camphor 4.0%

## Purpose

Organic Methyl Salicylate 10%.....................Topical Analgesic Organic Camphor 4.0%................................Topical Analgesic

## Uses

Temporarily relieves minor aches and pains of muscles and joints associated with simple backache, strains and bruises.

## Warnings

For external use only When using this product avoid contact with eyes do not apply to wounds or damaged skin Stop use and ask a doctor if condition worsens symptoms persist for more than 7 days or clear up and occur again within a few days If pregnant or breastfeeding , ask a health professional before use. Keep this out of the reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center immediately.
