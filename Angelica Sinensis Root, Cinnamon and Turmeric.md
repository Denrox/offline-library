# Angelica Sinensis Root, Cinnamon and Turmeric

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Drug class:** Non-Standardized Food Allergenic Extract  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Glucosamine Chondroitin Health Patch

## Active ingredients

turmeric 2% Chinese angelica 1% cinnamon 1%

## Purpose

Treat people suffering from neck, shoulder and back pain, as well as leg pain, including problems such as intervertebral disc protrusion, muscle strain and bone spurs.

## Uses

Apply it to the uncomfortable area, and leave it on for 3-5hours each time.

## Warnings

This is a topical patch meant for external use only.Do not ingest it.Keep out of reach of children to prevent accidental consumption.
