# Black Cohosh, Egg Phospholipids, Epinephrine, Niacinamide, Sepia Officinalis Juice, Strychnos Ignatii Seed and Sus Scrofa Ovary

> **Note:** Official U.S. prescribing information, written for health professionals; very long sections are shortened. Take prescription medicines only as prescribed, and never start, stop or change a dose without asking your doctor or pharmacist. Not medical advice.

**Route:** oral  
**Drug class:** alpha-Adrenergic Agonist, beta-Adrenergic Agonist, Catecholamine  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Brand and product names:** Total Tri-estro

## Uses

For the temporary relief of menopausal symptoms, menstrual disorders, hot flashes, depression, sleep disorders, fatigue, apathy. (**)

## Dosage

DIRECTIONS: Take 6 drops orally 4 times a day. Adults only.

## Warnings

- Stop use and call a doctor if symptoms persist or worsen.
- If pregnant or breast-feeding, consult a healthcare professional before use.
- Keep out of reach of children.
- Contains alcohol: in case of accidental overdose, consult a poison control center immediately
- Keep out of reach of children.
