# Throat Disorders

*Also called: Pharyngeal disorders*

Your throat is a tube that carries food to your esophagus and air to your windpipe and larynx. The technical name for your throat is the pharynx.

Throat problems are common. You've probably had a [sore throat](Sore%20Throat.md). The cause is usually a viral infection, but other causes include allergies, infection with [strep](Streptococcal%20Infections.md) bacteria or the leaking of stomach acids back up into the esophagus, called [GERD](GERD.md).

Other problems that affect the throat include:

- [Tonsillitis](Tonsillitis.md) - inflammation of the tonsils
- [Cancer](Throat%20Cancer.md)
- [Croup](Croup.md) - inflammation, usually in small children, which causes a barking cough
- Laryngitis - swelling of the voice box, which can cause a hoarse voice or loss of voice

Most throat problems are minor and go away on their own. Treatments, when needed, depend on the problem.
