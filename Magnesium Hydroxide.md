Magnesium hydroxide 2400 mg

## Purpose

Antacid / Saline Laxative

## Uses

as Antacid

- relieves
- heartburn
- sour stomach
- acid indigestion
- and upset stomach associated with these symptoms Uses as Saline Laxative
- for relief of occasional constipation (irregularity)
- this product generally produces bowel movement in 1/2 to 6 hours
