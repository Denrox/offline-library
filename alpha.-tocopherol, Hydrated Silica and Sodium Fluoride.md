# .alpha.-tocopherol, Hydrated Silica and Sodium Fluoride

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** oral  
**Ingredient forms:** .alpha.-tocopherol Acetate  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Rorobell Wawa Gum Care Tooth

## Active ingredients

Hydrated Silica 19.0% Sodium Fluoride 0.32% Tocopheryl Acetate 0.05%

## Purpose

Anti-plaque Anti-cavity Anti-gingivitis

## Uses

- Keeps teeth white and strong.
- Keeps the oral cavity clean.
- Refreshes the oral cavity.
- Helps prevent cavities with fluoride.
- Removes bad breath.
- Enhances the aesthetic effect.
- Helps prevent gingivitis, periodontitis (pyorrhea), and periodontal disease.
- Removes dental plaque (anti-plaque).

## Warnings

- The fluoride concentration of this toothpaste is 1,450 ppm.
- Do not swallow. Rinse your mouth thoroughly after use.
- If irritation or any abnormality occurs, such as damage to the gums or inside of the mouth, discontinue use and consult a physician or dentist.
- For children 6 years of age and under, use only a pea-sized amount under adult supervision to minimize swallowing. Do not allow the child to suck or swallow the toothpaste.
- If a child 6 years of age and under accidentally swallows a large amount, consult a physician or dentist immediately.
- Keep out of reach of children 6 years of age and under.
- This toothpaste contains more than 1,000 ppm fluoride. Consult a physician or dentist before use by children 6 years of age and under.

## Directions

- Brush teeth thoroughly with an appropriate amount of toothpaste.
