# Urea

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 6

**Also sold as:** Ishancare Cracked Skin Cream, Urevia, Avenaster Fungal Nail Care Duo Set (day), Urevia Plus

## Active ingredients

Urea 20%

## Purpose

Softens thickened, hardened and brittle cuticles; dissolves dead old keratin.

## Uses

Softens thickened keratin, dissolves dead cuticles, moisturizes and repairs, helps improve thickened toenails, brittle nails, and dry, peeling skin around the nails caused by tinea pedis, and enhances the penetration of subsequent antibacterial ingredients.

## Warnings

For external use only.

### Do not use

Do not use on children under 2 years of age unless directed by a doctor.

### Ask a doctor before use if

Ask Doctor Stop use and ask a doctor if irritation occurs, there is no improvement within 4 weeks (for athlete's foot and ringworm).

### When using this product

When Using When using this product, avoid contact with eyes.

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

Stop Use Stop use and ask a doctor if irritation occurs, there is no improvement within 4 weeks (for athlete's foot and ringworm).

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep Out Of Reach Of Children Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

## Directions

1.Clean and dry the affected area with soap and water. 2.Apply a thin layer of the Day Formula evenly to the nail surface. 3.Allow the product to absorb fully. 4.For thick nails, gently file the surface every 2‑3 days if needed. Do not over‑file or apply excessive pressure. 5.Use once daily after waking. Continue daily use to help soften thick nails and improve absorption

## Other information

Store at room temperature 15°C~30°C(59°F~86°F). Close cap tightly after each use. Keep away from direct sunlight and excessive moisture.

## Inactive ingredients

Purified Water, Glycerin, Propylene Glycol, Lactic Acid, Polyethylene Glycol, Tea Tree Oil
