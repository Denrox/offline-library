## Purpose

This is a topical cream that contains bio-identical estriol and estradiol derived from wild yam and formulated in an 80:20 ratio. When applied to the skin it can help balance your hormones and provide temporary relief from menopausal symptoms such as hot flashes.

## Uses

When applied to the skin it can help balance your hormones and provide temporary relief from menopausal symptoms such as hot flashes. DIRECTIONS: Apply once or twice a day.
