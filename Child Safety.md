# Child Safety

As parents, we want to keep our children safe from harm. Take steps to keep your children safe:

- Install the right child safety seat in your [car](https://medlineplus.gov/motorvehiclesafety.html)
- Teach children how to cross the street safely
- Make sure they wear the right gear and equipment for [sports](https://medlineplus.gov/sportssafety.html)
- Install and test smoke alarms
- Store medicines, cleaners and other [dangerous substances](https://medlineplus.gov/poisoning.html) in locked cabinets
- Babyproof your home
- Don't leave small children unattended
