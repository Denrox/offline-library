# Occupational Health for Health Care Providers

Health care workers are exposed to many job hazards. These can include :

- Infections
- Needle injuries
- Back injuries
- Allergy-causing substances
- Violence
- Stress

Follow good job safety and injury prevention practices. They can reduce your risk of health problems. Use protective equipment, follow [infection control](Infection%20Control.md) guidelines, learn the right way to lift heavy objects, and find ways to manage stress.

National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health
