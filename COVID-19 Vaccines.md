# COVID-19 Vaccines

[COVID-19](COVID-19%20%28Coronavirus%20Disease%202019%29.md) (coronavirus disease 2019) is an illness caused by a virus. This virus is a coronavirus called SARS-CoV-2. Most people with COVID-19 have mild symptoms, but some people become very sick. You can protect yourself by getting a COVID-19 vaccine. The vaccines might help keep you from getting sick from COVID-19. And if you do get sick, the vaccines can reduce your risk for:

- Needing to go to urgent care or the emergency room
- Getting severe illness
- Being hospitalized
- Dying
- Getting [long COVID](Post-COVID%20Conditions%20%28Long%20COVID%29.md)

It's important to stay up to date with the COVID-19 vaccines. Contact your health care provider if you don't know when you need another vaccine or if you have other questions about the vaccines.
