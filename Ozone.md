# Ozone

Ozone is a gas. It can be good or bad, depending on where it is. "Good" ozone occurs naturally about 10 to 30 miles above the Earth's surface. It shields us from the sun's ultraviolet rays. Part of the good ozone layer is gone. Man-made chemicals have destroyed it. Without enough good ozone, people may get too much ultraviolet radiation. This may increase the risk of skin cancer, cataracts, and immune system problems.

"Bad" ozone is at ground level. It forms when pollutants from cars, factories, and other sources react chemically with sunlight. It is the main ingredient in [smog](https://medlineplus.gov/airpollution.html). It is usually worst in the summer. Breathing bad ozone can be harmful. It can cause coughing, throat irritation, worsening of [asthma](https://medlineplus.gov/asthma.html), [bronchitis, and emphysema](https://medlineplus.gov/copd.html). It can lead to permanent lung damage, if you are regularly exposed to it.
