# Ascorbic Acid and Zinc Oxide

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Drug class:** Vitamin C  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Vita Soluball

## Active ingredients

Ascorbic acid 200mg, Zinc oxide 40mg per 1000mg

## Purpose

Antioxidation, Antiinflammatory on the skin

## Uses

Sprinkle on the affected area of the skin and rub it. Once a day - below 100mg per day

## Warnings

Stop using it immediately if there are signs of skin rashes or hypersensitivity.

### Keep out of reach of children

KEEP OUT OF REACH OF THE CHILDREN

## Directions

for topical use only

## Inactive ingredients

silicon dioxide
