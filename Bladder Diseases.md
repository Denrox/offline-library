# Bladder Diseases

The bladder is a hollow organ in your lower abdomen that stores urine. Many conditions can affect your bladder. Some common ones are:

- Cystitis - inflammation of the bladder, often from an [infection](Urinary%20Tract%20Infections.md)
- [Urinary incontinence](Urinary%20Incontinence.md) - loss of bladder control
- [Overactive bladder](Overactive%20Bladder.md) - a condition in which the bladder squeezes urine out at the wrong time
- [Interstitial cystitis](Interstitial%20Cystitis.md) - a chronic problem that causes bladder pain and frequent, urgent urination
- [Bladder cancer](Bladder%20Cancer.md)

Doctors diagnose bladder diseases using different tests. These include [urine tests](Urinalysis.md), x-rays, and an examination of the bladder wall with a scope called a cystoscope. Treatment depends on the cause of the problem. It may include medicines and, in severe cases, surgery.
