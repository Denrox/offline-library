# Miconazole Nitrate (cutaneous)

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** cutaneous  
**Labels on file:** 3

**Also sold as:** Happy Nuts Jock Itch Powder, Terpenicol Antifungal Cream, Terpenicol Antifungal Solution

## Active ingredients

Miconazole Nitrate 2% Purpose Antifungal

## Purpose

Antifungal

## Uses

for the cure of most jock itch (tinea cruris). Relieves itching, burning, cracking, scaling and discomfort associated with jock itch.

## Warnings

For external use only. Do not use on children under 2 years of age unless directed by a doctor. When using this product avoid contact with the dyes. Stop use and ask a doctor if irritation occurs there is no improvement within 2 weeks Keep out of reach of children. If product is swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

## Directions

Clean affected area thoroughly. Apply a thin layer of the product over the affected area twice daily (morning and night) or as directed by a doctor Supervise children when using this product Use daily for 2 weeks If condition persists longer, consult a doctor This product is not effective on the scalp or nails

## Inactive ingredients

Corn Starch, Fragrance, Potassium Sorbate, Rice Starch, Sodium Benzoate
