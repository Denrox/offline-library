# Mentha Piperita Leaf

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Nano Eye Drop Serum

## Purpose

DIRECTIONS OF SAFE USE: Wash your hands before using eye drops, and tilt your head back or lie flat as much as possible. Pull down the lower eyelid and separate it from the eyeball, and drop the eye drops into the lower conjunctival fornix. Close your eyes for at least five minutes, or gently massage the upper and lower tear ducts at the inner corner of the eye with your fingers. Use a tissue or towel to wipe off the drops and tears that have flowed out of the eye.

## Uses

Relieve eye fatigue; maintain overall eye comfort; promote vision recovery; make vision clearer and more comfortableensitivity.

## Warnings

Please keep out of reach of children. Do not swallow.Please clean your hands before use to ensure the best results from the product. Discontinue use if signs of irritation or rash occur. Store in a cool and dry place.
