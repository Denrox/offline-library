Warnings:

### When using this product

When using this product

- keep out of eyes,ears,and mouth.
- If product gets in eyes,flush with water for 15 minutes. Ask a doctor before you use If you have diabetes or poor blood circulation. Directions
- Wash feet thoroughly with warm water,and gently file the thickened part of the fungal nail with a nail until it's flat.
- Apply a layer of the liquid to the surface of the fungal nail and allow it to be absorbed into the nail.
- Repeat the process twice a day.Use it continuously for at least 4weeks to see the effect. And if you want complete improvement,we recommend using it for 6 weeks or more.
