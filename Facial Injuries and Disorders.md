# Facial Injuries and Disorders

Face injuries and disorders can cause pain and affect how you look. In severe cases, they can affect sight, speech, breathing and your ability to swallow. [Fractures](Fractures.md) (broken bones), especially in the bones of your nose, cheekbone and [jaw](Jaw%20Injuries%20and%20Disorders.md), are common facial injuries.

Certain diseases also lead to facial disorders. For example, nerve diseases like [trigeminal neuralgia](Trigeminal%20Neuralgia.md) or [Bell's palsy](Bell%27s%20Palsy.md)sometimes cause facial pain, spasms and trouble with eye or facial movement. Birth defects can also affect the face. They can cause underdeveloped or unusually prominent facial features or a lack of facial expression. [Cleft lip and palate](Cleft%20Lip%20and%20Palate.md) are a common facial birth defect.
