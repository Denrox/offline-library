**Route:** topical

**Also sold as:** Vigority Wild Yam Cream, Woodsleep Wild Yam Cream

## Active ingredients

DIOSCOREA VILLOSA (WILD YAM) ROOT EXTRACT

## Purpose

1. Helps regulate hormone levels, relieve menopausal symptoms and reduce premenstrual syndrome. 2. Reduce mood swings and anxiety and improve quality of life. 3. Promote deep sleep, relieve fatigue and reduce insomnia.

## Uses

After cleansing and drying skin, apply appropriate amount to palms. Gently massage belly, chest, arms and inner thighs until completely absorbed. It is recommended for menstruating women to start using it one week before menstruation, twice daily until the end of menstruation.

## Warnings

Please keep out of reach of children. Do not swallow.Please clean your hands before use to ensure the best results from the product. Discontinue use if signs of irritation or rash occur. Store in a cool and dry place.
