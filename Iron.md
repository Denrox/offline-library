# Iron

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Real Summer

## Active ingredients

IRON 76%

## Purpose

Insect bite

## Uses

(s)

- For temporary relief of pain and itching associated with insect bites.

## Warnings

For external use only Keep out of reach of infants and young children, and be careful not to eat or swallow. If there is a rash or itching during use, remove it immediately. If you repeatedly attach and detach the patch, the adhesive strength will be weakened, so be careful directions

### Keep out of reach of children

KEEP OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN Keep out of reach of infants and young children, and be careful not to eat or swallow.

## Directions

- Put Real Summer patch on the insect bite

## Inactive ingredients

ACTIVATED CHARCOAL, SODIUM CHLORIDE, VERMICULIN
