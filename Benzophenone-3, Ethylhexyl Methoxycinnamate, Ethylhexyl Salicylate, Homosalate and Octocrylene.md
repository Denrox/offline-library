## Active ingredients

Octocrylene 10% Homosalate 10% Ethylhexyl Methocinnamate 7.5% Ethylhexyl Salicylate 5% Benzophenone-3 4%

## Purpose

Sunscreen

## Uses

helps prevent sunburn if used as directed with other sun protection measures (see directions), decreases the risk of skin cancer and early aging caused by the sun

## Warnings

For external use only. Do not use on damaged or broken skin Avoid contact with eyes. If contact occurs, flush eyes thoroughly with cool water. If adverse reaction occusrs, discontinue use and ask a physician Keep out of reach of children.

### Do not use

## Directions

Shake well before use. Spray liberally onto skin 15 minutes before sun exposure and spead evenly with hands. Reapply every 2 hours and immediately after swimming.
