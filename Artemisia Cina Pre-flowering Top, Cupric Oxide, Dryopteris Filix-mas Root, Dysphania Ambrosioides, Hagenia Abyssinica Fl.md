## Uses

USES: Temporarily relieves symptoms caused by parasites and worms such as bloating, itching and intestinal dysfunction.** ** ​This statement has not been evaluated by the FDA. It is based on documented Homeopathic Materia Medica.

## Dosage

DIRECTIONS: Shake 10 times before use. Adults & children above 12 years of age: spray 3-4 times under the tongue 2-3 times a day, or as directed by a health care professional. Children under 12: use half the adult dose.

## Warnings

IF PREGNANT OR BREAST-FEEDING, ask a health professional before use. If symptoms worsen or persist for more than a week, discontinue use and contact a doctor. ​KEEP OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN.
