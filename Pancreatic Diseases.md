# Pancreatic Diseases

The pancreas is a gland behind your stomach and in front of your spine. It produces juices that help break down food and hormones that help control [blood sugar](https://medlineplus.gov/bloodglucose.html) levels. Problems with the pancreas can lead to many health problems. These include:

- [Pancreatitis](https://medlineplus.gov/pancreatitis.html), or inflammation of the pancreas: This happens when digestive enzymes start digesting the pancreas itself
- [Pancreatic cancer](https://medlineplus.gov/pancreaticcancer.html)
- [Cystic fibrosis](https://medlineplus.gov/cysticfibrosis.html), a genetic disorder in which thick, sticky mucus can also block tubes in your pancreas

The pancreas also plays a role in diabetes. In [type 1 diabetes](https://medlineplus.gov/diabetestype1.html), the beta cells of the pancreas no longer make insulin because the body's immune system has attacked them. In [type 2 diabetes](https://medlineplus.gov/diabetestype2.html), the pancreas loses the ability to secrete enough insulin in response to meals.
