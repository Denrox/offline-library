The **Radio Amateurs' Emergency Network**, also known as **RAYNET**, is a British national voluntary communications service provided by [amateur radio operators](Amateur%20radio%20operator.md). It was formed in 1953 and exists to supplement national communication channels in the event of an emergency. The capitalised word RAYNET is now a registered trademark of the RAYNET-UK organisation.
