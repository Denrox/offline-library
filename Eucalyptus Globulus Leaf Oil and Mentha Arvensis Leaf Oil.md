## Active ingredients

MENTHA ARVENSIS LEAF OIL、EUCALYPTUS GLOBULUS LEAF OIL

## Purpose

Using a variety of plant essential oils, it can reduce the number of microorganisms and make shoes and feet emit a fragrant plant aroma.

## Uses

Spray the spray directly on your shoes or feet to make odors disappear quickly

## Warnings
