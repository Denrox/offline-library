## Purpose

Hair regrowth treatment

## Uses

Regenerate scalp hair

## Warnings

For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. If contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water. Do not use if you are allergic to any of the ingredients. Discontinue use if irritation occurs. Consult your doctor before use if you are taking medication, pregnant, or breastfeeding.

Keep out of reach of children.

## Directions

Thoroughly wet your hair. Pour a coin-sized amount of shampoo into your palm. Apply the shampoo from roots to tips. Lather and gently massage your scalp with your fingertips for 2-3 minutes. Rinse thoroughly with warm water. To effectively support hair growth, use this shampoo at least three times per week, and pair it with a hair growth serum.

## Other information

Store in a cool, dry place.
