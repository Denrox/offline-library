# Salicylic Acid (cutaneous, extracorporeal, percutaneous)

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** cutaneous, extracorporeal, percutaneous  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Juananan Salicylic Acid Essence

## Active ingredients

SALICYLIC ACID 1.4%

## Purpose

Clear acne and breakouts

## Uses

* Clear acne and breakouts * Moisturize and soothing * Strengthen skin barrier

## Warnings

For external uses only Do not use on damaged or broken skin Avoid eye contact when using, if contact occurs, rinse with water to remove Stop use if rash and irritation occur Keep out of reach of children.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children.

## Directions

Apply an appropriate amount evenly to face and neck after cleansing

AQUA、 BUTYLENE GLYCOL、 NIACINAMIDE、 ZEA MAYS (CORN) STARCH、 ICODEXTRIN、 XANTHAN GUM、 1,2-HEXANEDIOL、 PENTYLENE GLYCOL、 YDROXYACETOPHENONE、 MADECASSOSIDE、 BAROSMA BETULINA LEAF OIL、 PURSLANE、 CENTELLA ASIATICA WHOLE、 ASIATICOSIDE、 CITRIC ACID
