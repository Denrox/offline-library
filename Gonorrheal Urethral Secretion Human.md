# Gonorrheal Urethral Secretion Human

> **Note:** Official U.S. prescribing information, written for health professionals; very long sections are shortened. Take prescription medicines only as prescribed, and never start, stop or change a dose without asking your doctor or pharmacist. Not medical advice.

**Route:** oral  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Brand and product names:** Medorrhinum

## Uses

Use Rx only*

## Dosage

Directions 5 pellets 3 times a day until symptoms are relieved.

## Warnings

Stop use and ask a doctor if symptoms persist for more than 3 days or worsen. If pregnant or breast-feeding, ask a health professional before use. Keep out of reach of children.
