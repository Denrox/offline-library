# Amanita Muscaria Fruiting Body

> **Note:** Official U.S. prescribing information, written for health professionals; very long sections are shortened. Take prescription medicines only as prescribed, and never start, stop or change a dose without asking your doctor or pharmacist. Not medical advice.

**Route:** oral  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Brand and product names:** Agaricus Muscarius

## Uses

USE: To be used according to standard homeopathic indications.

## Dosage

DIRECTIONS: Dissolve 3-5 pellets under the tongue 3 times a day or as directed by a professional.

## Warnings

Keep out of reach of children. If pregnant or breast-feeding, ask a health care professional before use. Stop use and ask a health care professional if symptoms persist for more than 3 days or worsen. Keep out of reach of children.
