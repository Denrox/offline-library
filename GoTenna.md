# GoTenna

goTenna
- Type Privately held company
- Founded November 2012 (2012-11)
- Founders Daniela Perlein, Jorge Perdomo
- Headquarters

United States

- Brands goTenna, goTenna Mesh, goTenna Pro, goTenna Plus
- Website gotenna.com

**goTenna** (goTenna Inc.) is a technology company that creates mesh networking devices that pair with smartphones, allowing users to send texts and share locations on a peer-to-peer basis without needing active cell networks

### goTenna Pro

In March 2017, the company announced its goTenna Pro line, for [professional mobile radio](Professional%20mobile%20radio.md) communications needs, leaving the consumer market for government markets. To finance its pivot, the company raised $24M in Series C equity and debt funding in 2019
