# Juniperus Sabina Leafy Twig

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** oral  
**Labels on file:** 3

## Active ingredients

SABINA

## Purpose

USES To relieve the symptoms of minor joint pain.

## Uses

Indications: SABINA Minor joint pain

## Warnings

STOP USE AND ASK DOCTOR If symptoms persist/worsen or if pregnant/nursing, consult your practitioner.

### Keep out of reach of children

KEEP OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN Keep this and all medicines out of reach of children.

## Directions

Adults: 4 drops into a tsp. of water 3 times a day. Children: 1/2 dose. Repeat at greater intervals as condition subsides. Or as directed bya lic. practitioner.

## Inactive ingredients

Sucrose/Lactose
