## Purpose

Purpose: Arnica Montana 50M...........................Bruising, muscle strain Hypericum Perforatum 50M................Pain Ledum Palustre 50M...........................Swelling, pain Rhus toxicodendron 50M....................Sprains, strains, swelling, pain Ruta graveolens 50M..........................Strains

## Uses

For the reduction of swelling and pain associated with joint sprains, muscle strains, shin splints, dislocations, overuse injuries, and bruising.

## Warnings

Warnings For external use on intact skin only Use only as directed. Do not bandage tightly. Stop use and ask a doctor if condition worsens. Do not use in the eyes, over open wounds, or if you have unexpected bleeding. If pregnant or breast-feeding, ask a health professional before use. Do not use if you are allergic to any of this product's active or ingredients.
