### Section II – Terms

#### battalion aid station

The forward-most medically staffed treatment location organic to the maneuver battalion. (ATP 4-02.4).

#### *care under fire

The first phase of care in tactical combat casualty care where very limited care is provided for the casualty while the enemy threat is still active. Also known as CUF or care under threat.

#### casualty

Any person who is lost to the organization by having been declared dead, duty status – whereabouts unknown, excused absence-whereabouts unknown, missing, ill, or injured. (JP 4-02).

#### casualty collection point

A location that may or may not be staffed, where casualties are assembled for evacuation to a medical treatment facility. (ATP 4-02.2).

#### casualty evacuation

(Joint) The unregulated movement of casualties aboard any vehicle. Also called CASEVAC. (JP 4-02). (Army) The movement of casualties aboard nonmedical vehicles or aircraft without en route medical care. (ATP 4-02.13).

#### *casualty response

The immediate and organized care, evacuation and reporting of those injured or killed in action.

#### *combat lifesaver

(Joint) Nonmedical Department of Defense person who has received additional trauma training and equipment, providing enhanced medical treatment beyond self-aid/buddy aid. (JP 4-02).

(Army) A Soldier trained and equipped to provide enhanced first aid as a secondary mission, known as CLS.

#### *first aid

The immediate lifesaving measures performed on casualties or military working animals.

#### first responder

Is anyone who provides initial and immediate treatment to self or others. (JP 4-02).

#### medical evacuation

The timely and effective movement of the wounded, injured, or ill to and between medical treatment facilities on dedicated and properly marked medical platforms with en route care provided by medical personnel. Also called MEDEVAC. (ATP 4-02.2).

#### medical treatment facility

(Joint) A facility established for the purpose of furnishing medical and/or dental care to eligible individuals. Also called MTF. (JP 4-02).

(Army) Any facility established for the purpose of providing medical treatment. (FM 4-02).

#### patient

(Joint) A sick, injured, wounded, or other person receiving medical care, dental care, or other medical treatment. (JP 4-02).

(Army) A patient is a wounded, ill and injured Soldier who receives medical care or treatment from medically trained personnel. (ATP 4-02.10).

#### tactical combat casualty care

The deliberate integration of tactics and medicine to treat casualties during combat. Also called TCCC. (JP 4-02).

#### *tactical evacuation care

The third phase of care in tactical combat casualty care. Care provided during transportation from the battelefield to a medical treatment facility.

#### *tactical field care

The second phase of care in tactical combat casualty care. Care provided once the threat has been neutralized, the scene is safe or the casualty has moved or been moved out of the immediate threat situation. Also known as TFC.

#### triage

Triage is the system of sorting and prioritizing casualties based on the tactical situation, mission, and available resources (FM 4-02).
