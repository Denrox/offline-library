**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 2

**Also sold as:** Varicose Vein Massage, Koxuo Arnica Bruise Cream

## Active ingredients

5% ARNICA

## Purpose

pain relief

## Uses

For the temporary relief of localized pain, itching, or discomfort from minor skin irritations.

## Warnings

For external use only.

### Do not use

Do not use on wounds or damaged skin

### Ask a doctor before use if

Ask a doctore before use If pregnant or breast-feeding ask a health professional before use.

### When using this product

When Using Avoid contact with eyes.

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

Stop Use if irritation or rash develops

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep Out Of Reach Of Children If swallowed, get medical help.

## Directions

Apply a small amount of Arnica cream to the affected area 2~3 times daily, Gently massage until fully absorbed.
