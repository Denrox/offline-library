# .alpha.-tocopherol, Hydrogen Peroxide and Silica

**Route:** oral  
**Ingredient forms:** .alpha.-tocopherol Acetate  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Rorobell Wawa Whitening Tooth

## Active ingredients

Silica 7.0% Hydrogen Peroxide (35%) 2.14% Tocopheryl Acetate 0.2%

## Purpose

Anti-plaque Whitening Anti-gingivitis

## Uses

- Helps whiten teeth.
- Helps prevent cavities.
- Helps eliminate bad breath.
- Helps strengthen teeth.
- Helps keep the oral cavity clean.
- Helps freshen the oral cavity.
- Helps improve the appearance of teeth.
- Helps prevent gingivitis and periodontitis (pyorrhea).
- Helps prevent periodontal disease.
- Helps prevent gum disease.
- Helps remove dental plaque (anti-plaque).

## Warnings

Precautions for Use

- Do not use this product for any purpose other than brushing teeth.
- If a large amount of this product is accidentally swallowed, follow the instructions of a pharmacist, dentist, or physician. Storage Instructions
- Keep out of reach of children. Children should use this product only under adult supervision.
- Avoid direct sunlight. Keep the cap tightly closed and store at room temperature. Whitening Toothpaste Warnings
- Avoid using this product if you have mouth sores, severe cavities, or severe periodontal disease, as it may cause significant irritation.
- If your gums become red or sore after use, discontinue use, as this whitening toothpaste may not be suitable for you.
- This product contains hydrogen peroxide as its whitening active ingredient. The tube may swell due to the release of oxygen generated by hydrogen peroxide. This is a natural phenomenon and does not affect the quality of the product. You may also hear a sound as carbon dioxide escapes from the tube during use.

## Directions

- Brush teeth thoroughly with an appropriate amount of toothpaste.
