## Active ingredients

Povidone Iodine 10% (available iodine 1%)

## Purpose

Topical antiseptic

## Uses

First aid topical antiseptic to help prevent infection in minor cuts scrapes abrasions

## Warnings

For external use only. do not get in eyes

### Do not use

on animal bits in or near the eyes or over large portions of the body

### Ask a doctor before use if

Ask a dcotor before use if you have deep or puncture wounds serious burns

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

redness, irritation, swelling or pain persists or increases infection occurs

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children . If swallowed, get medical help immediately or contact a Poison Control Center right away. 1-800-222-1222
