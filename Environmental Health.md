# Environmental Health

Our environment affects our health. If parts of the environment, like the [air](Air%20Pollution.md), [water](Water%20Pollution.md), or soil become polluted, it can lead to health problems. For example, asthma pollutants and chemicals in the air or in the [home](Indoor%20Air%20Pollution.md) can trigger asthma attacks.

Some environmental risks are a part of the natural world, like [radon](Radon.md) in the soil. Others are the result of human activities, like [lead poisoning](Lead%20Poisoning.md) from paint, or exposure to [asbestos](Asbestos.md) or [mercury](Mercury.md) from mining or industrial use.

NIH: National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences
