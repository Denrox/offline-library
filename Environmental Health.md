# Environmental Health

Our environment affects our health. If parts of the environment, like the [air](https://medlineplus.gov/airpollution.html), [water](https://medlineplus.gov/waterpollution.html), or soil become polluted, it can lead to health problems. For example, asthma pollutants and chemicals in the air or in the [home](https://medlineplus.gov/indoorairpollution.html) can trigger asthma attacks.

Some environmental risks are a part of the natural world, like [radon](https://medlineplus.gov/radon.html) in the soil. Others are the result of human activities, like [lead poisoning](https://medlineplus.gov/leadpoisoning.html) from paint, or exposure to [asbestos](https://medlineplus.gov/asbestos.html) or [mercury](https://medlineplus.gov/mercury.html) from mining or industrial use.

NIH: National Institute of Environmental Health Sciences
