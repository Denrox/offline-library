## Purpose

1. Contains tea tree oil, which can eradicate fungi and relieve foot fungal problems. 2. Use in multiple scenarios, can be used on feet, socks and shoes. 3. ## Uses

1. Completely clean and dry foot skin 2. Take an appropriate amount of this product and spray it on your feet, shoes or socks 3. Wait 2-3 minutes for your feet to fully absorb it.
