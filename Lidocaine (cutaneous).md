## Active ingredients

Lidocaine 4%

## Purpose

Local Anesthetic

## Uses

For the temporary relief of muscle pain and joint pain associated with: -Backache -Cramps -Soreness -Strains -Discomfort -Neckache

## Warnings

For External Use Only Allergy Alert Local anesthetics may cause methemoglobinemia. If an allergic reaction occurs, stop use and seek medical attention immediately.
