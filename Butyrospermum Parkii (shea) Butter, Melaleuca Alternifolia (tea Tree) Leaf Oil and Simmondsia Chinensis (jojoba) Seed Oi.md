## Active ingredients

BUTYROSPERMUM PARKII (SHEA) BUTTER、SIMMONDSIA CHINENSIS (JOJOBA) SEED OIL、MELALEUCA ALTERNIFOLIA (TEA TREE) LEAF OIL

## Purpose

1. Helps soften thick, rough, and hardened skin. 2. Gently exfoliates dry, flaky, and uneven texture. 3. Helps improve the appearance of cracked heels and calluses. 4. Moisturizes deeply to leave skin feeling smoother and softer. 5. With salicylic acid, lactic acid, and ceramides to support smoother-looking skin.

## Uses

on feet, heels, elbows, knees, or other rough areas. Wash hands after use.

## Warnings

For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. Do not use on broken, irritated, or sensitive skin. Discontinue use if irritation or rash occurs. Keep out of reach of children. Do not swallow.
