## Warnings

Do not use if you have kidney disease Ask a doctor or pharmacist before use if you are

- taking a prescription drug. Antacids may interact with certain prescription drugs.
- if you are on a sodium-restricted diet When using this product
- do not take more than 16 teaspoonful in 24 hours
- do not use the maximum dosage for more than 2 weeks
- laxative effect may occur Keep out of reach of children. In case of overdose, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center immediately at 1-800-222-1222.
