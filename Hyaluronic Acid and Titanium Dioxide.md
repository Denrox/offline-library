# Hyaluronic Acid and Titanium Dioxide

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Eelhoe Body Coverage Perfector

## Active ingredients

TITANIUM DIOXIDE、HYALURONIC ACID

## Purpose

Easily covers sun damage, veins, scars, uneven skin tone and more. Brightens the skin with a natural glow. Transfer, water and sweat resistant. Lasts for days when applied to exfoliated, oil - free skin.

## Uses

1. Cleanse the skin. 2. Apply liberally On the areas you want to touch up, using a hand or body brush, working upwards and outwards. Allow product to dry for 10 minutes, and if desired, apply another coat for added coverage, allowing each coat to dry for 10 minutes. 3. Remove excess product by wiping the covered area with a paper towel, towel or glove until the product no longer comes off. Allow product to fully set for 10 minutes before touching clothing or linens.

## Warnings

Please keep out of reach of children. Do not swallow.Please clean your hands before use to ensure the best results from the product. Discontinue use if signs of irritation or rash occur. Store in a cool and dry place.
