## Warnings

For External use only. Use only as directed. Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

- More than one patch on your body at a time
- On cut, irritated or swollen skin
- On puncture wounds
- For more than one week without consulting a doctor
- If you are allergic to any active or inactive ingredients
- If pouch is damaged or opened. If pregnant or breast feeding Contact a physician prior to use. WHEN USING:
- Use only as directed
- Read and follow all directions and warnings on this carton
- Do not allow contact with the eyes
- Do not use at the same time as other topical analgesics
- Do not bandage tightly or apply local heat (such as heating pads) to the area of use
- Do not microwave
- Dispose of used patch in manner that always keeps product away from children and pets. Used patches still contain the drug product that can produce serious adverse effects if a child or pet chews or ingests this patch. Stop use and consult a doctor if
- Condition worsens
- Redness is present
- Irritation develops
- Symptoms persist for more than 7 days or clear up and occur again within a few days
- You experience signs of skin injury, such as pain, swelling or blistering where the product was applied.

## Directions

Adults and children 12 years of age and over: Clean and dry affected area. Carefully remove backing from patch starting at a corner. Apply sticky side of patch to affected area. Use one patch for up to 12 hours. Discard after single use. Children under 12 years of age: consult a physician.

## Other information

Store in a clean, dry place outside of direct sunlight. Protect from excessive moisture.
