**Also sold as:** Aoao Rapid Acne Relief

## Active ingredients

BENZOYL PEROXIDE,Salicylic Acid

## Purpose

Acne Spot Treatment

## Uses

Clears Acne & Bumps,Fast Flattens Acne,Fades Acne Scars,Acne Treatment.

- Clears up acne pimples, blemishes, and blackheads and allows skin to heal.
- Helps prevent the formation of new acne blemishes and pimples.
- Clean the skin thoroughly before applying this product.
- Cover the entire affected area with a thin layer one to three times daily.

## Warnings

Do not use if you

- Have very sensitive skin.
- Are sensitive to benzoyl peroxide or salicylic acid. For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes。 Avoid contact with hair and dyed fabrics, including clothes, towels, and bedding, which may be bleached by benzoyl peroxide.

### Do not use

It is recommended to test the product behind the ear before use. If discomfort is felt, do not use.
