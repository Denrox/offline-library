# Women's Health

Women have unique health issues. And some of the health issues that affect both men and women can affect women differently.

Unique issues include [pregnancy](Pregnancy.md), [menopause](Menopause.md), and conditions of the female organs. Women can have a healthy pregnancy by getting early and regular [prenatal care](Prenatal%20Care.md). They should also get recommended breast cancer, [cervical cancer](Cervical%20Cancer%20Screening.md), and [bone density](Bone%20Density.md) screenings.

Women and men also have many of the same health problems. But these problems can affect women differently. For example:

- Women are more likely to die following a [heart attack](Heart%20Attack.md) than men
- Women are more likely to show signs of [depression](Depression.md) and [anxiety](Anxiety.md) than men
- The effects of [sexually transmitted infections](Sexually%20Transmitted%20Infections.md) (STIs) can be more serious in women
- [Osteoarthritis](Osteoarthritis.md) affects more women than men
- Women are more likely to have [urinary tract problems](Urine%20and%20Urination.md)
