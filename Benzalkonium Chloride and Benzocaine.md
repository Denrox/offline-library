## Active ingredients

Benzalkonium Chloride 0.1% Benzocaine 4%

## Purpose

First Aid Antiseptic Topical Anesthetic

## Uses

First aid for temporary relief of pain and itching and to help prevent infection in minor cuts, scrapes, burns

## Warnings

For external use only. Flammable Do not use near heat, flame, or fire, or while smoking Allergy Alert do not use this product if you have a history of allergy to local anesthetics such as procaine, benzocaine, or other "caine" anesthetics

### Do not use

in the eyes over large areas of the body

### Ask a doctor before use if

you have deep or puncture wounds animal bites serious burns

### When using this product

avoid contacting with eyes

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

condition worsens or symptoms persists for more then 7 days symptoms clear up and occur again with a few days

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or call a Poison Control Center right away

## Directions

For adults and children 2 yeas of age or older: gently wipe the affacted area 1-3 times daily with a clean cloth Children under 2 yeas of age: consult a doctor

## Other information

Store at room temperature 20-25 C (68-77 F). Avoid direct sunlight

## Inactive ingredients

Poloxamer, PEG-400, Glycerin, NaCl, Water
