# Aluminum Zirconium Octachlorohydrate Anhydrous

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 2

**Also sold as:** Secret Delicate Rose Clear, Secret Cool Waterlily Clear

## Active ingredients

Aluminum zirconium octachlorohydrex Gly 16% (anhydrous)

## Purpose

Antiperspirant

## Uses

reduces underarm wetness

## Warnings

For external use only. Do not use on broken skin Ask a doctor before use if you have kidney disease Stop use if rash or irritation occurs Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
