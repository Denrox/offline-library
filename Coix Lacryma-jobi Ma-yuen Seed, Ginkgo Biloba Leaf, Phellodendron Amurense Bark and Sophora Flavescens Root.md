## Purpose

1. Help the skin reduce the influence of foreign objects, gently remove warts on the skin, reduce the appearance of warts, and make the skin smoother. 2. The texture is light and non-sticky, the ingredients are mild, and it protects the skin around warts from being affected by warts. 3. Suitable for all types of skin, suitable for removing moles, spots, skin tags, etc.

## Uses

1. Wash the skin tag area and dry thoroughly. 2. Gently rotate the end of the product to dispense the liquid into the brush applicator. 3 Apply a thin layer of product directly over the skin tag 2-3 times daily until the tag falls off. 4.Expose the area for dry thoroughly.
