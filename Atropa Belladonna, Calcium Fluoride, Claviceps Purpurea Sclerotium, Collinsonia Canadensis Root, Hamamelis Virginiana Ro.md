# Atropa Belladonna, Calcium Fluoride, Claviceps Purpurea Sclerotium, Collinsonia Canadensis Root, Hamamelis Virginiana Root Bark/stem Bark, Horse Chestnut, Milk Thistle, Pulsatilla Pratensis Whole and Vipera Berus Venom

> **Note:** Official U.S. prescribing information, written for health professionals; very long sections are shortened. Take prescription medicines only as prescribed, and never start, stop or change a dose without asking your doctor or pharmacist. Not medical advice.

**Route:** oral  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Brand and product names:** Venoforce Ii

## Uses

For temporary relief of achiness and minor swelling of the lower extremities, aching and soreness in limbs and tired feeling in legs and tenderness, and achiness from distended veins.

## Dosage

DIRECTIONS: 10 drops orally, 3 times a day. Consult a physician for use in children under 12 years of age.

## Warnings

If pregnant or breast-feeding, ask a health care professional before use. Keep out of reach of children. In case of overdose, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away. Do not use if tamper evident seal is broken or missing. Store in a cool, dry place.
