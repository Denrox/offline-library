# Appendix B: Rescue Equipment

> **Note:** U.S. Army manual written for soldiers; parts of it assume combat conditions (Tactical Combat Casualty Care). Figures are not included. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

## Warrior Aid and Litter Kit

B-1. The warrior aid and litter kit (WALK) (figure B-1) allows first aid treatment and immediate evacuation of one patient in a CASEVAC vehicle and treatment of multiple trauma patients at the POI. The total weight of the WALK can weigh approximately 29 pounds, 15 ounces. The WALK offers an option for a vehicle life-saving kit.

**Figure B-1. Warrior aid and litter kit**

B-2. The system holds a quad-fold litter (see figure B-2 on page 225) and a pocket system containing medical supplies. The WALK contains a hypothermia prevention kit to help stabilize patients during transport. The WALK also includes the following:

- Five pairs of nitrile trauma gloves.

- Two NPAs, size 28 French with lubricant.

- One casualty equipment bag.

- Two CoTCCC approved occlusive chest seals.

- Two combat application TQs.

- Six trauma dressings, six inches.

- Four rolls of rolled gauze, 4.5 inches by 4.1 yards.

- One abdominal emergency trauma dressing.

- Two moldable fracture splints.

- One pair of trauma shears, 7.25 inches.

- One roll surgical tape, two inches.

- One role multipurpose tape.

- Six polycarbonate eye shields.

- Two TCCC cards.

- One aviation panel (Recognition, Orange).

- Four tie-down straps (universal litter).

**Figure B-2. Quad-fold litter**

B-3. The WALK fits into any standard or nonstandard CASEVAC asset and is useful as part of the prepositioning of medical supplies or cache on a forward operating base, CCP, or at a patrol base.

## Polymer Flexible Litter System

B-4. The CoTCCC approved polymer flexible litter system (figure B-3 on page 226) uses a sled design to allow the evacuator to continue returning fire as the casualty is evacuated. It is used for confined space, high angle, or technical rescue, and it provides casualty protection while allowing the aid and litter teams to have their hands free for weapons and security.

B-5. It is equipped for horizontal hoisting by helicopter or vertical hoisting in caves or industrial confined spaces. When the patient is packaged, the litter becomes rigid. It is rolled for storage in a backpack, which is included with the system. The litter system weighs approximately 17 pounds.

**Figure B-3. Casualty evacuation using the polymer flexible litter system**
