## Active ingredients

## Purpose

Topical Analgesic First Aid Antiseptic

## Uses

Relieves stinging and itching caused by mosquito bites, minor burns, and abrasions

## Warnings

For external use only. Do not use If allergic to certain ingredients In eyes, mouth, and mucous membranes Stop use and ask the doctor if condition worsens or persists for more than 7 days or clears up and occurs again within a few days. Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact Poison Control center immediately.
