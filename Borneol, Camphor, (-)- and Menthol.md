# Borneol, Camphor, (-)- and Menthol

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Mqupin Tiger Patch

## Active ingredients

Menthol 2.0% Borneol 1.2% Camphor 1.5%

## Purpose

topical Analgesic

## Uses

for sub healthy people with discomfort caused by neck, shoulder, waist and leg.

## Warnings

For single use only. Do not swallow. Do not use on allergic skin and wounds. This product is not a substitute for medicine. Stop use if skin irritation such as rash, or redness occurs. Stop use and ask a doctor if conditions worsen.

### Do not use

Do not swallow. Do not use on allergic skin and wounds.

### Ask a doctor before use if

Ask Doctor Stop use and ask doctor if

- rash, itching or excessive skin irritation develops
- condition worsens
- symptoms persist for more than 7 days
- symptoms clear up and occur again within a few days If pregnant or breastfeeding, ask a health professional before use
- If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away

### When using this product

When Using When using this product

- use only as directed
- do not bandage tightly or use with a heating pad
- avoid contact with eyes and mucous membranes
- do not apply to wounds or damaged, broken or irritated skin
- do not use at the same time as other topical analgesics

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

Stop Use Stop use and ask doctor if

- rash, itching or excessive skin irritation develops
- condition worsens
- symptoms persist for more than 7 days
- symptoms clear up and occur again within a few days If pregnant or breastfeeding, ask a health professional before use
- If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep Out Of Reach Of Children Keep out of reach of children Keep out of reach of children and pets.

## Directions

adults and children over 12 years:

- clean and dry the affected area
- remove backing from patch and apply patch to affected area
- use 1 patch per knee/Other body areas for 8 to 12 hours, once per day (It can stay activated for up to 12-72 hours depending on the applied areas. children 12 years or younger: ask a doctor

## Other information

store at room temperature, not to exceed 86°F (30°C)

## Inactive ingredients

Radix Angelicae Pubescentis,Drynaria Rhizome, Myrrh, Frankincense, Elderberry, Safflower, Angelicae, Hot melt adhesive release paper, non-woven fabric.
