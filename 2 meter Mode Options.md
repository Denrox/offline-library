# 2 meter Mode Options

*Tags: vhf · score 4*

## Question

FM seems to be the most popular in the 144 to 148 MHz Amateur Band .Narrow band is used for spectrum conservation . SSB is also used but is more common on the HF bands .Would SSB provide any range advantage when taking VHF propagation into Account ?

## Answer (score 5, by ON5MF Jurgen)

As you can read in this answer: [Single Side Band AM vs FM](Single%20Side%20Band%20AM%20vs%20FM.md), SSB is more power efficient than FM so normally you should get a greater range using the same power from your transmitter. But as R2AUK stated you probably will find a lot more people to talk to on 2m FM than on 2m SSB.
