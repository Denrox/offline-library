# Epinephrine and Lidocaine

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** cutaneous  
**Drug class:** Amide Local Anesthetic, Antiarrhythmic, alpha-Adrenergic Agonist, beta-Adrenergic Agonist, Catecholamine  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Tattoo

## Active ingredients

Lidocaine 5%, Epinephrine 1%

## Purpose

Topical pain relief and wound repair

## Uses

As directed by the physician. Usable RangeMcro Needle Pain Tattooing Body Piercing,Laser Tattoo Removal, Laser Hair Removal, Waxing,Permanent Cosmetics.

### Do not use

Under 18 must be accompanied by an adult

### When using this product

Keep away from your eyes when using this product. If in contact with eyes, wash eyes thoroughly with water.

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

Stop using and ask your doctor if you have any irritation or rash. These may be signs of a serious illness. Please stay away from children.If you swallow, seek medical attention immediately

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach for children.

## Directions

Usage 1. Rotate the nozzle with the top of the cap. Resealed after use. 2.Wash the skin with soap and warm water making sure It's clean and free of bacteria. 3.Put the anesthetic evenly on the skin(1/8 inch or2-3mm thick), and cover the skin with a plastic wrap for 50-60 minutes toachieve 6-10 hours of numbness. 4.Before starting the operation,Remove the cream and let the skin breathe for 5-10minutes before proceeding with the work.When using this product, please control the room temperatue at 28C ~30C,so that the skin can absorb the cream quicker.

## Other information

Storage Store above Zero and Below30°℃. Protect from Light. Do not freeze.

## Inactive ingredients

Prilccaine, Carbomer, Propylene Glycol, Triethanolamine
