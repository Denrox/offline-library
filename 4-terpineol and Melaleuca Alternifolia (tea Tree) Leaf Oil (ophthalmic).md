# 4-terpineol and Melaleuca Alternifolia (tea Tree) Leaf Oil (ophthalmic)

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** ophthalmic  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Eye Carespray

## Active ingredients

4-Terpineol0.002% Melaleuca Alternifolia (Tea Tree) Leaf Oil0.001%.

## Purpose

Antibacterial

## Uses

Gently refreshes and hydrates the eye area. Helps soothe dry, tired eyes and remove surface impurities-leaving skin around the eyes feeling clean, comfortable, and moisturized.

## Warnings

.For external use only. ·This product is for daily use only and does not have disease prevention or treatment functions. It cannot be used as a substitute for medicines, health care products, cosmetics, etc.

### Do not use

People with allergic constitution, those with rashes or open wounds around the eyes are prohibited from using.

### When using this product

When Using When using this product, please remove contact lenses before use. In case of uncomfortable contact, rinse thoroughly with water.

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

Stop Use Stop application if eyes or skin around eyes show signs of redness, irritation or sensitivity and consult your eye care professional.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep Oot Of Reach Of Children To avoid the risk of choking and suffocation, keep bottle and plastic bag away from babies, children and pets.

## Directions

.Wash your hands & clean the eye area; Spray on each closed eye or a clean wipe, 2~3times; Wipe gently with a clean cloth to dry along the base of both lash line. ·Children under 6 years of age should be surpervised when using this product.

## Other information

·Store below 77°F/25℃. ·Store in a cool, dry place, protected from the direct sunlight. ·Validity period for 3 years.

## Inactive ingredients

Water, Phenoxyethanol, Glycerin, Aloe Barbadensis Leaf Extract, XanthophyIl, Taurine, Cyanocobalamin, Sodium Hyaluronate
