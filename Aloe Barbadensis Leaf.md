# Aloe Barbadensis Leaf

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 2

**Also sold as:** Eelhoe Bodyacne Removal Cleanser, Invisible Fit Sun Cream

## Active ingredients

ALOE BARBADENSIS EXTRACT

## Purpose

1. Protects against UV rays and reduces photodamage 2. Replenishes skin moisture and improves dryness 3. Maintains skin hydration and enhances radiance 4. Soothes skin discomfort and increases tolerance

## Uses

1. 15-20 minutes before going out, apply sunscreen evenly to the parts of your face and body that need sun protection, massage until fully absorbed and a natural film is formed. 2. If you need to put on makeup, please use it before putting on makeup. If you are exposed to the sun for a long time, you need to reapply every 2-3 hours.

## Warnings

Please keep out of reach of children. Do not swallow.Please clean your hands before use to ensure the best results from the product. Discontinue use if signs of irritation or rash occur. Store in a cool and dry place.
