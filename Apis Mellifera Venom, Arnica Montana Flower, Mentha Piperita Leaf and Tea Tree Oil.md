## Purpose

Eliminates skin tags, warts and moles; relieves dryness, redness and sensitivity caused by inflammation; promotes skin growth and repair.

## Uses

Clean and dry skin, apply appropriate amount of this product to the affected area, massage for absorption.

## Warnings
