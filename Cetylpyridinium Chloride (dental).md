## Active ingredients

Cetylpyridinium Chloride 0.075%

## Purpose

Antigingivitis/Antiplaque

## Uses

helps prevent and reduce plaque and gingivitis helps control plaque bacteria that contribute to the development of gingivitis, an early form of gum disease, and bleeding gums

## Warnings

Stop use and ask a dentist if gingivitis, bleeding, or redness persist for more than two weeks you have painful or swollen gums, pus from the gum line, loose teeth, or increasing spacing between the teeth. These may be signs or symptoms of periodontitis, a serious form of gum disease. Keep out of reach of children under 6 years of age. If more than used for rinsing is accidentally swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
