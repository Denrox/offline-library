# Aloe Vera Leaf, Edetate, Glycerin and Urea

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Ingredient forms:** Edetate Disodium  
**Drug class:** Non-Standardized Chemical Allergen  
**Labels on file:** 2

**Also sold as:** Miracle Footcream Lavender, Miracle Footcream Lemon

## Active ingredients

urea, glycerin, aloe barbadensis leaf extract, disodium edta

## Purpose

dead skin cell care cream

## Uses

taking off an appropriate amount you apply to your rough feet as you massage two or three times a day

## Warnings

don’t use on the part where there is injury, eczema, or dermatitis if swallowed, get medical help or contact a person control center immediately

### Keep out of reach of children

keep out of reach of the children

## Directions

for topical use only

## Inactive ingredients

Water, macadamia ternifolia seed oil, stearic acid, shea butter, etc
