## Active ingredients

Capsaicin 0.025% Menthol 10% Methyl Salicylate 15% Purpose: Topical Analgesic

## Purpose

Topical Analgesic

## Uses

For the temporary relief of minor aches and pains of muscles and joints, associated with arthritis, strains, bruises, and sprains.
