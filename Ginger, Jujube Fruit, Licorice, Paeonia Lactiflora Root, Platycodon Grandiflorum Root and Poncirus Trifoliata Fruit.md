# Ginger, Jujube Fruit, Licorice, Paeonia Lactiflora Root, Platycodon Grandiflorum Root and Poncirus Trifoliata Fruit

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** oral  
**Drug class:** Non-Standardized Plant Allergenic Extract, Non-Standardized Food Allergenic Extract  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Baenosin

## Active ingredients

platycodon root, licorice, jujube, peony root, ginger, poncirus immature fruit

## Purpose

pyrogenic diseases with redness, swelling, stiffness and pain

## Uses

adults take 2 capsules 3 times a day before meals or between meals

## Warnings

do not take this medicine if you have following symptoms symptoms of headaches, chills, and fever other than local symptoms such as spasm (red with blood), swelling, and pain at the same time

### Keep out of reach of children

keep out of reach of the children

## Directions

for oral use only

## Inactive ingredients

lactose monohydrate, acacia, corn starch, carboxymethylcellulose calcium, l-menthol
