# Avobenzone, Homosalate, Octisalate, Octocrylene and Petrolatum

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 2

**Also sold as:** Chapstick Moisturizer Cool Mint, Chapstick Moisturizer Original

## Active ingredients

Avobenzone 3.0% Homosalate 4.0% Octisalate 4.5% Octocrylene 2.6% White Petrolatum 36.9%

## Purpose

Sunscreens Skin protectant

## Uses

- helps prevent sunburn
- helps prevent and temporarily protects chafed, chapped or cracked lips
- helps prevent and protect from the drying effects of wind and cold weather

## Warnings

Skin Cancer/ Skin Aging Alert: Spending time in the sun increases your risk of skin cancer and early skin aging. This product has been shown only to help prevent sunburn, not skin cancer or early skin aging. For external use only Do not use on damaged or broken skin Stop use and ask a doctor if rash occurs When using this product keep out of eyes. Rinse with water to remove. Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

## Directions

- apply liberally 15 minutes before sun exposure
- reapply at least every 2 hours
- use a water resistant sunscreen if swimming or sweating
- children under 6 months of age: Ask a doctor

## Other information

- store at 20-25°C (68-77°F)
- protect this product from excessive heat and direct sun
