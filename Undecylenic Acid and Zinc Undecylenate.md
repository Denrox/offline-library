# Undecylenic Acid and Zinc Undecylenate

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 8

**Also sold as:** Derman Antifungal, Tea Tree Oil Antifungal Foot Cream, Tea Tree Oil Antifungal Cream, Everfere Antifungal, Velloryn Antifungal Cream, Antifungal Cream, Apuree Prorevive Maximum Strength Antifungal, Hongocura

## Active ingredients

UNDECYLENIC ACID 5% ZINC UNDECYLENATE 20%

## Purpose

Anti-fungal

## Uses

- relieves itching, burning, cracking, and scaling associated with athlete’s foot, jock itch, and ringworm
- helps relieve symptoms associated with athlete’s foot, jock itch, and ringworm

## Warnings

For external use only

### Do not use

- on children under 2 years of age unless directed by a doctor.

### When using this product

- avoid contact with the eyes

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

- irritation occurs
- there is no improvement within 4 weeks (for athlete’s foot & ringworm) or within 2 weeks (for jock itch)

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away at 1-800-222-1222.

## Directions

- adults and children 2 years and older: apply a thin layer to the affected area 2-3 times daily
- children under 2 years: do not use; ask a doctor

## Inactive ingredients

Water,Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride, Cetyl Alcohol, Glycerin, Melaleuca Alternifolia (Tea Tree) Leaf Oil, Panthenol, Thymus Vulgaris (Thyme) Flower/Leaf Oil, Centella Asiatica Extract, Menthol, Aloe Vera Extract, Ceramide NP, Colloidal Oatmeal, Rosemary, Urea, Sodium Hyaluronate, Cocos Nucifera (Coconut) Oil, Lavandula Angustifolia (Lavender) Oil, Eucalyptus Globulus (Eucalyptus) Leaf Oil, Geranium
