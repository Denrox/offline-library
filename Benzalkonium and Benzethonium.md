# Benzalkonium and Benzethonium

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Ingredient forms:** Benzethonium Chloride, Benzalkonium Chloride  
**Labels on file:** 2

**Also sold as:** Cpap Mask Cleaning Wipes, Anzoon Antiseptic Wipes

## Active ingredients

Benzethonium Chloride 0.05% Benzalkonium Chloride 0.05%

## Purpose

Antimicrobial

## Uses

Hand sanitizer to help reduce bacteria that potentially can cause disease.

## Warnings

For external use only When using this product Do not use on open skin wounds Do not use Do not use in or near the eyes. In case of contact, rinse eyes thoroughly with water. Stop use and ask a doctor Stop use and ask a doctor if irritation or rash appears and lasts. Keep out of reach of children If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
