# Flash ESP32 Devices

> **Info:**
>
> The recommended method for firmware flashing is the Web Flasher.

## Flashing Method for ESP32 Devices

1. The Web Flasher requires either Chrome or Edge browsers but is an excellent choice for quickly flashing devices. **This method is highly recommended for firmware flashing, especially for new users of the project, as it is easy to use.**
2. The [CLI Script](Flashing%20with%20the%20CLI.md) is considered the "manual process" for flashing firmware.
3. Flashing your device using an [external serial adapter](Flashing%20with%20an%20External%20Serial%20Adapter.md) should only be attempted as a last resort if no other method has been successful.

> **note:**
>
>
> The [web client](Web%20Client%20Overview.md) at meshtastic.local is only updated with a full wipe and reinstall of the device. If you choose a reinstall, you will get the latest (bundled) web interface.  To preserve your settings, you may [export your configuration](Meshtastic%20Python%20CLI%20Guide.md) prior to a reinstall and load them back after.
