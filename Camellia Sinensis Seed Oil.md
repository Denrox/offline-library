## Purpose

Fast Easy Effective in 5 days

## Uses

Wipe the affected area clean, take out this product and apply it evenly on the affected area. Remember not to apply it on healthy skin. If there is a layer of hard skin on the surface of the affected area, you can trim it slightly and apply it to facilitate penetration.

## Warnings

Please keep out of reach of children. Do not swallow.Please clean your hands before use to ensure the best results from the product. Discontinue use if signs of irritation or rash occur. Store in a cool and dry place.
