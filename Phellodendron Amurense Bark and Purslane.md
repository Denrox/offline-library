# Phellodendron Amurense Bark and Purslane

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 2

**Also sold as:** Wart Remover, Wart Remover Cream

## Active ingredients

Purslane(0.3%) Phellodendron Bark(0.2%)

## Purpose

Wart Remover

## Uses

Suitable for subhealthy individuals experiencing localized discomfort caused by plantar warts, filiform warts, digitate warts, common warts,flat warts, seborrheic keratosis (senile warts), corns, etc.

## Warnings

For external use only.

### When using this product

Avoid contact with eyes and mouth. Keep out of reach of children to prevent accidental ingestion.

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

Discontinue use and consult a physician if allergic reactions or discomfort occur.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children to prevent accidental ingestion.

## Directions

Clean the affected area, apply an appropriate amount to the discomforted area until absorbed.Recommended usage: 2-3 times daily.

## Inactive ingredients

ISATIS LEAF DICTAMNUS ROOT BARK EQUISETUM HYEMALE SAFFLOWER PEACH SEED RED PEONY ROOT PEG-400 GLYCERIN WHITE PETROLEUM JELLY STEARYL ALCOHOL CARBOMER BORNEOL CAMPHOR PURIFIED WATER
