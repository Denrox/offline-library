# Bryonia Alba Root, Colchicum Autumnale Bulb, Kalmia Latifolia Leaf, Ledum Palustre Twig, Lycopodium Clavatum Spore, Rhododendron Aureum Leaf and Toxicodendron Pubescens Leaf

> **Note:** Official U.S. prescribing information, written for health professionals; very long sections are shortened. Take prescription medicines only as prescribed, and never start, stop or change a dose without asking your doctor or pharmacist. Not medical advice.

**Route:** oral  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Brand and product names:** Stiff Joints Hp

## Uses

USES: Temporarily relieves red, hot, swollen joints, stiff joints, cracking in joints, &/or stitching pain.**

## Dosage

DIRECTIONS: Adults and children above 12 years: 10 drops orally 3 times daily, or as directed by a health care professional.

## Warnings

Consult a physician for use in children under 12 years of age. IF PREGNANT OR BREAST-FEEDING , ask a health care professional before use. KEEP OUT OF THE REACH OF CHILDREN . In case of overdose (or accidental ingestion) get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away. Do not use if TAMPER EVIDENT seal is broken or missing.
