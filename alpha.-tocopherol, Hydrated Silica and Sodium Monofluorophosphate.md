# .alpha.-tocopherol, Hydrated Silica and Sodium Monofluorophosphate

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** oral  
**Ingredient forms:** .alpha.-tocopherol Acetate  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Rorobell Wawa Kids Tooth Natural Green Grape Flavor

## Active ingredients

Hydrated Silica 13.0% Sodium Fluoride 0.22% Tocopheryl Acetate 0.20%

## Purpose

Anti-plaque Anti-cavity Anti-gingivitis

## Uses

- Keeps teeth white and strong.
- Keeps the oral cavity clean.
- Refreshes the oral cavity.
- Prevents cavities by fluoride and removes bad breath.
- Enhances the aesthetic effect.
- Helps prevent gingivitis, periodontitis (pyorrhea), periodontal disease, and gum disease.
- Prevention of tartar deposition.
- Removes dental plaque (anti-plaque).

## Warnings

- This toothpaste contains 1,000 ppm fluoride.
- Stop use and ask a doctor or dentist if irritation or other adverse reactions occur in the gums or mouth.
- If more than the amount used for brushing is accidentally swallowed by a child under 6 years of age, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
- Do not swallow. Rinse mouth thoroughly after brushing.
- Children under 6 years of age should use only a pea-sized amount under adult supervision to minimize swallowing.
- Keep out of reach of children under 6 years of age

## Directions

- Brush teeth thoroughly with an appropriate amount of toothpaste.

Sorbitol, Water, Glycerin, Hydroxyapatite, Sodium Cocoyl Glutamate, Cellulose Gum, Natural Green Grape Flavor, Stevioside, Sodium Bicarbonate, Xylitol, Sodium Hyaluronate, Allyl Isothiocyanate, Camellia Sinensis Leaf Extract, Salvia Officinalis Leaf Extract, Aloe Barbadensis Leaf Extract, Citrus Paradisi Fruit Extract, Calendula Officinalis Flower Extract, Pearl Powder
