Diphenhydramine hydrochloride 2.0% Menthol 1.0%

P urpose Diphenhydramine Hydrochloride External analgesic l-Menthol External analgesic

## Uses

temporarily relieves pain and itching associated with

- minor skin irritations
- insect bites
- minor cuts
- scrapes
- rashes due to poison ivy
- sunburn
- minor burns
