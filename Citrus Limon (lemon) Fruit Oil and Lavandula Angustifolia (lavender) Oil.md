## Active ingredients

LAVANDULA ANGUSTIFOLIA (LAVENDER) EXTRACT、CITRUS LIMON (LEMON) FRUIT EXTRACT

## Purpose

Continuous use will effectively improve sleep quality

## Uses

1. Remove the anti sticking layer. 2. Stick the glue surface on the sleeping point. 3. Tear it off when you get up (about 8-12 hours).

## Warnings

Please keep out of reach of children. Do not swallow.Please clean your hands before use to ensure the best results from the product. Discontinue use if signs of irritation or rash occur. Store in a cool and dry place.
