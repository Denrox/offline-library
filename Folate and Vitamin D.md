# Folate and Vitamin D

> **Note:** Official U.S. prescribing information, written for health professionals; very long sections are shortened. Take prescription medicines only as prescribed, and never start, stop or change a dose without asking your doctor or pharmacist. Not medical advice.

**Route:** oral  
**Ingredient forms:** Folate Sodium  
**Labels on file:** 2

**Brand and product names:** Foltira, Folvyris

## Uses

Folvyris is for patients with unique nutritional needs requiring increased folate levels and Vitamin D supplementation due to Vitamin D deficiency.

## Dosage

Take one tablet daily or as directed by a licensed medical practitioner. If you are pregnant or nursing ask a healthcare professional.

## Warnings

Keep out of reach of children.

## Precautions

Folvyris should be administered under the supervision of a licensed medical practitioner.
