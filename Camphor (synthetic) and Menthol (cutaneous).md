# Camphor (synthetic) and Menthol (cutaneous)

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** cutaneous  
**Labels on file:** 3

**Also sold as:** Orthonesic Pain Relieving Gel, Dr. Sana Alivio Total Alivio Total Roll On, Dr Sana Alivio Total Alivio Total

## Active ingredients

Camphor 3 % Menthol 1.25 %

## Purpose

Camphor 3%.......Topical Analgesic Cream Menthol 1.25 %....Topical Analgesic Cream

## Uses

Temporarily relieves minor aches and pains of muscles and joints associated with simple backache, arthritis, strains, bruises and sprains.

## Warnings

For external use only. Avoid contact with the eyes. Flammable: Keep away from excessive heat or open flame.

### Do not use

On wounds or damaged skin With a heating pad On a child under 2 years of age with this product

### When using this product

Avoid contact with eyes or mucous membranes Do not bandage tightly Do not apply to irritated skin

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

Condition worsens Symptoms persist for more than 7 days Symptoms clear up and occur again within a few days Excessive skin irritation occurs

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

## Directions

Adults and children 2 years and older: apply to affected areas not more than 3 to 4 times daily. Children under 2 years: ask a doctor

## Inactive ingredients

Water (Aqua), Methyl Salicylate, Glycerin, Cetearyl Alcohol, Mineral Oil (Paraffinum Liquidum), Glyceryl Stearate, Eucalyptus Globulus Leaf Oil, Polysorbate 20, Phenoxyethanol, Xanthan Gum, Disodium EDTA, Tocopheryl Acetate, Arnica Montana Flower Extract, Triethanolamine.
