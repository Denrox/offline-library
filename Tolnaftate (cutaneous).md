# Tolnaftate (cutaneous)

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** cutaneous  
**Labels on file:** 2

**Also sold as:** Multi-purrose Nail, Seebeellar Tolnaftate 1% Antifungal Body Wash

## Active ingredients

(s) Tolnaftate 1% (w/w)

## Purpose

Antifungal

## Uses

1. For the treatment and prevention of common fungal skin infections (athlete's foot, ringworm, jock itch, and tinea versicolor) when used as part of your daily shower routine. 2. Temporarily relieves itching, scaling, burning, redness, and discomfort associated with these fungal conditions, including tinea versicolor. 2. Cleanses skin thoroughly while delivering antifungal benefits.

## Warnings

For external use only

### Do not use

Do not use on scalp or mucous membranes (eyes, nose, mouth, genital areas). If contact with eyes occurs, rinse thoroughly with water. Do not use on children under 2 years of age unless directed by a doctor. If pregnant or breastfeeding, consult a healthcare professional before use. Do not use if you areallergic to any ingredient in this product.

### When using this product

·Avoid contact with eyes ·If irritation occurs, please stop using

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

- Irritation, redness, or rash develops or worsens. - No improvement in symptoms after 4 weeks for athlete's foot, ringworm, or tinea versicolor, or after 2 weeks for jock itch. - The infection spreads or becomes severe.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center immediately.

## Directions

1. Use daily during shower or bath 2. Wet skin thoroughly, lather into foam, apply to affected areas, and massage gently 3. Leave foam on skin for 1-2 minutes, then rinse thoroughly 4. For best results, use consistently every day

## Other information

Store at room temperature(15°-30°C /59°-86°F) Protect from excessive heat and direct sunlight
