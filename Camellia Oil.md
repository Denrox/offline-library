## Active ingredients

Camellia Oil 0.3%

## Purpose

USES First aid to help prevent bacterial contamination or skin infection, and fortemporary relief of pain and itching associated with minor burns, scaldssunburns, cuts, and scrapes.

## Uses

Directions For adults and children 2 years ofage or older. Clean the affected area.Apply a small amount of the ointment to the affected area, not more than3 to 4 times daily. For children under 2 years ofage, ask a doctor before use.

## Warnings

For external use only. When using this product do not get into eyes. Stopuse and ask a doctor if your condition worsens, does not improve, persistsin more than 7 days, or occurs again after a few days. Consult a doctorbefore using on animal bites or serious burns. Keep out ofreach of childrenf swallowed, call poison control or seek medical help.
