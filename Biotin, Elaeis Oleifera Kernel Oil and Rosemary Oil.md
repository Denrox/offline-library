# Biotin, Elaeis Oleifera Kernel Oil and Rosemary Oil

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Batana Oil

## Active ingredients

Batana Oil 60% Rosemary Oil 0.50% Biotin 0.50%

## Purpose

Promotes Hair Growth

## Uses

Crowds with hair loss, damaged hair or sparse hair

## Warnings

For external use only. If swallowed, seek immediate medical assistance.

### Do not use

the product on children under 10 years of ageunless directed by a doctor.

### When using this product

Refrain from applying the product on injured or swollen skin areas. For individuals with sensitive skin, a patch test on the wrist's interior is recommended prior to use, ensuring an irritation-free experience. Avoid if allergic to nuts. If irritation occurs, discontinue use and consult a physician. For external use only.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children.lf swallowed,get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center(1-800-222-1222) right away.

## Directions

1.Apply 2-3drops to the scalp of the affected area,sectioning hair or into the palm of your hand 2. Massage the oil into the scalp and hair with fingertips. 3.Leave on overnight or minimum 30 minutes before showering.

## Inactive ingredients

Castor Oil Pumpkin Seed Oil Vitamin E
