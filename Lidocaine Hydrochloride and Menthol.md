## Active ingredients

(% w/w) Lidocaine HCL 4% Menthol 1%

## Purpose

Topical Anesthetic (Lidocaine HCL 4%) Topical Analgesic (Menthol 1%)

## Uses

For the temporary relief of pain and itching associated with:

- minor burns
- sunburn
- minor cuts
- scrapes
- insect bites
- minor skin irritations

## Warnings

For external use only

- Do not apply to wounds or damaged skin
- Do not bandage tightly When using this product
- Avoid contact with the eyes Stop use and ask a doctor if If condition worsens, or if symptoms persist for more than 7 days or clear up and occur again within a few days, discontinue use of this product and consult a physician.
