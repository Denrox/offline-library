# Aloe Barbadensis Leaf and Centella Asiatica Leaf

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 2

**Also sold as:** Akolkol Toenail Fungus Treatment, Ximonth Nail Fungus Care Liquid

## Active ingredients

ALOE BARBADENSIS LEAF EXTRACT、 CENTELLA ASIATICA LEAF EXTRACT

## Purpose

1. Helps restore the health of damaged nails, enhances the hardness of nails, reduces breakage and peeling, and makes nails stronger. 2. Help eliminate the discomfort and appearance problems caused by fungal infection of toenails and restore the healthy appearance of toenails. 3. It can provide nutrients to nails, thereby promoting the repair function of nails and gradually restoring the smoothness and luster of nails.

## Uses

1. Trim the affected toenail with a stainless steel nail file, clean and keep dry 2. Apply an appropriate amount of this product evenly to the entire nail surface 3. Wait for it to dry and allow it to be absorbed.

## Warnings

Please keep out of reach of children. Do not swallow.Please clean your hands before use to ensure the best results from the product. Discontinue use if signs of irritation or rash occur. Store in a cool and dry place.
