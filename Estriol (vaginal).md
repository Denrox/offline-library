# Estriol (vaginal)

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** vaginal  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Estriol Cream

## Active ingredients

Estriol 0.2%

## Purpose

Hormone Balance

## Uses

Helps maintain hormonal balance. May help ease symptoms associated with menopause.

## Warnings

For external use only

### Do not use

if pregnant, nursing, or trying to conceive.

### When using this product

Avoid contact with eyes. If contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water.

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

If irritation or rash develops, discontinue use and consult a health care provider.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, seek medical help or contact a Poison Control Center immediately.

## Directions

Take 2 pumps of this product onto your fingertips Apply cream evenly to the intimate area (inner labia and perineal region) Wait for 20 minutes until the cream is fully absorbed. Precautions Target audience: For adult women only.
