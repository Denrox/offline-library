# Sexual Health

Sexuality is a big part of being human. Love, affection and sexual intimacy all play a role in healthy relationships. They also contribute to your sense of well-being. A number of disorders can affect the ability to have or enjoy sex in both [men](Sexual%20Problems%20in%20Men.md) and [women](Sexual%20Problems%20in%20Women.md).

Factors that can affect sexual health include:

- Fear of unplanned pregnancy
- Concerns about [infertility](Infertility.md)
- [Sexually transmitted infections](Sexually%20Transmitted%20Infections.md) (STIs)
- Chronic diseases such as cancer or heart disease
- Medicines that affect sexual desire or performance
