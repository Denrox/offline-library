## Uses

BIOCORNEUM ® can reduce the appearance of scars and is ideal for use on any intact skin surface, including skin that flexes (such as joints) and areas exposed to sun (such as the face and hands).

## Warnings

Skin Cancer/Skin Aging Alert Spending time in the sun increases your risk of skin cancer and early skin aging. This product has been shown only to help prevent sunburn, not skin cancer or early skin aging. For external use only. Keep out of reach of children. Do not use on damaged or broken skin. When using this product keep out of eyes. Rinse with water to remove. Stop use and ask a doctor if rash occurs.

## Directions

Ensure that the affected area is clean and dry. Apply a thin, even layer twice daily. Allow to dry. Wait 15 minutes before sun exposure. Children under 6 months of age: Ask a doctor.

## Other information

Protect product in this container from excessive heat and direct sun.
