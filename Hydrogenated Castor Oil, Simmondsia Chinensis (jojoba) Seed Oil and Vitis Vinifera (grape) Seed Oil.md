**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Eelhoe Cuticle Pusher Pen

## Active ingredients

VITIS VINIFERA (GRAPE) SEED OIL、SIMMONDSIA CHINENSIS (JOJOBA) SEED OIL、HYDROGENATED CASTOR OIL

## Purpose

1. Easily peels off without damaging the nail edge, ensuring safer care. 2. Quickly creates a defined nail shape, enhancing the refinement and aesthetic of your manicure. 3. A great daily aid for clean, neat nails and healthy, radiant nail edges.

## Uses

1. Shake 2-3 times before use and let sit with the brush tip facing downward for 5-10 minutes. 2. Press the brush tip against the edge of your nail to gently infuse the essential oil. Gently push the brush tip along the edge of your nail to remove excess dead skin.

## Warnings

Please keep out of reach of children. Do not swallow.Please clean your hands before use to ensure the best results from the product. Discontinue use if signs of irritation or rash occur. Store in a cool and dry place.
