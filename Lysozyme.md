# Lysozyme

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** cutaneous  
**Labels on file:** 2

**Also sold as:** Bioshuga Dermomucosal Antibacterial Gel, Bioshuga Cutaneous Mucosal Antimicrobial Solution

## Active ingredients

(s) Lysozyme (18,000-22,000 U/mL)

## Purpose

Antibacterial agent

## Uses

Helps reduce bacteria on intact skin and mucosal surfaces Provides antibacterial protection for external use

## Warnings

For external use only Not a drug product; does not treat, cure, or prevent disease When used on vaginal mucosa, it should not be used for prevention of sexually transmitted diseases

### Do not use

Do not use if you are allergic to any of the ingredients

### When using this product

do not get into eyes, If contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water; Avoid using with incompatible (antagonistic) medications

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

lf an allergic reaction occurs, stop use immediately and consult a doctor.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

## Directions

Apply an appropriate amount to the affected area Leave on for at least2 minutes
