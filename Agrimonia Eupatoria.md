# Agrimonia Eupatoria

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** oral  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** 1912herb Co Heart Well Herb

## Active ingredients

Agrimony 20%, Gogi Berry 8%, Angelica gigas root 8%, Licorice 8%, Lilyturf 8%

## Purpose

digestive enhancement, relief of stomach pain, improvement of digestive function

## Uses

Take 1 pouch (3g) three time a day after a meal

## Warnings

If you have an unusual or allergic constitution, check the ingredients before consuming.

### Keep out of reach of children

keep out of reach of the children

## Directions

for oral use only

## Inactive ingredients

Poria Cocos 8%, Cnidium 8%, Turmeric 8%, Paeoniae Radix Alba 8%, Jujube 8%, Ginger 8%
