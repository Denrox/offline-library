# 20-meter band

The **20-meter** or **14-MHz** [amateur radio](Amateur%20radio.md) band is a portion of the shortwave radio spectrum, comprising frequencies stretching from 14.000 MHz to 14.350 MHz. The 20-meter band is widely considered among the best for long-distance communication (DXing), and is one of the most popular—and crowded—during contests. Several factors contribute to this, including the band's large size, the relatively small size of antennas tuned to it (especially as compared to antennas for the [40-meter band](40-meter%20band.md) or the [80-meter band](80-meter%20band.md)) and its good potential for daytime international operation even in unfavorable [propagation](Radio%20propagation.md) conditions.

#### Canada

Canada is part of region 2 and as such is subject to the IARU band plan. Radio Amateurs of Canada offers the bandplan below as a recommendation for use by radio amateurs in that country but it does not have the force of law and should only be considered a suggestion or guideline.

- License class   14.000–14.070   14.070–14.095   14.095–14.0995   14.0995–14.1005   14.1005–14.112   14.112–14.350
- Basic(+), Advanced
