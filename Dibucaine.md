# Dibucaine

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Drug class:** Standardized Chemical Allergen  
**Labels on file:** 6

**Also sold as:** Cvs Topical Anesthetic Hemorrhoidal, Cvs Topical Analgesic Hemorrhoidal, Dibucaine Topical Anesthetic 1% Hemorrhoidal

## Active ingredients

Dibucaine 1%

## Purpose

Hemorrhoidal Ointment

## Uses

Temporarily relieves pain and itching due to hemorrhoids or other anorectal disorders.

## Warnings

For External use only Allergy alert Certain persons can develop allergic reactions to ingredients in this product. If the symptoms being treated do not subside or if redness, irritation, swelling, pain, or other symptoms develop or increase, discontinue use and ask a doctor.

### Do not use

In or near eyes In children under 2 years of age

### When using this product

Do not use more than directed unless directed by a doctor Do not put this product into the rectum by using fingers or any mechanical device or applicator.

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

Bleeding occurs Condition worsens or does not improve within 7 days

### Pregnancy or breast-feeding

If Pregnant or Breast-Feeding Ask a health care professional before use.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of the reach of Children If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

## Directions

Adults and children 12 years of age or older. If possible, clean the affected area with mild soap and warm water and rinse thoroughly. Dry by patting or blotting with toilet tissue or a soft cloth before applying. Apply externally to the affected area up to 3 to 4 times daily. Children under 12 years of age. ask a doctor

## Other information

Store at 20º-25ºC (68º-77ºF) T amper-evident; do not use if foil seal is broken or missing

## Inactive ingredients

Lanolin, Light Mineral Oil, Purified Water, White Petrolatum
