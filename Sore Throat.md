# Sore Throat

*Also called: Pharyngitis*

Your throat is a tube that carries food to your esophagus and air to your windpipe and larynx (also called the voice box). The technical name for the throat is pharynx.

You can have a sore throat for many reasons. Often, [colds](https://medlineplus.gov/commoncold.html) and [flu](https://medlineplus.gov/flu.html) cause sore throats. Other causes can include:

- [Allergies](https://medlineplus.gov/allergy.html)
- [Mononucleosis](https://medlineplus.gov/infectiousmononucleosis.html)
- [Smoking](https://medlineplus.gov/smoking.html)
- [Strep throat](https://medlineplus.gov/streptococcalinfections.html)
- [Tonsillitis](https://medlineplus.gov/tonsillitis.html)

Treatment depends on the cause. Sucking on lozenges, drinking lots of liquids, and gargling may ease the pain. Over-the-counter pain relievers can also help, but children should not take aspirin.
