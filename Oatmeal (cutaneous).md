# Oatmeal (cutaneous)

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** cutaneous  
**Drug class:** Non-Standardized Food Allergenic Extract, Non-Standardized Plant Allergenic Extract  
**Labels on file:** 2

**Also sold as:** Sel Ph Baby Ph-balanced Eczema Dryness Babylotion, Crogooe Skin Relief Cream

## Active ingredients

Colloidal Oatmeal

## Purpose

Temporarily protects and helps relieve minor skin irritation and itching due to eczema, rashes, and extremely dry skin.

## Uses

Relieve skin itching

## Warnings

For external use only Do notuse on deep wounds, puncture wounds, infected areas or burns. Avoid contact with eyes. If contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water. Stop use and consult a doctor if rash develops or irritation worsens. If symptoms do not improve within 7 days, see a healthcare provider. Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or call Poison Control Center right away. If pregnant or breast-feeding, consult a health professional prior to use.

### Keep out of reach of children

Please keep out of reach of children

## Directions

Apply generously to affected area as needed, preferably after bathing or showering
