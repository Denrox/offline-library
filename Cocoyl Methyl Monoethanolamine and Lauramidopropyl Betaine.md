# Cocoyl Methyl Monoethanolamine and Lauramidopropyl Betaine

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** extracorporeal  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Foaming Hand

## Active ingredients

(s) Sodium Laureth Sulfate, Cocamide Methyl MEA.

## Purpose

Antimicrobial

## Uses

APPLYASMALL AMOUNT ONTO WET HANDS. WORK INTO A LATHER AND RINSE.

## Warnings

FOR EXTERNAL USE ONLY. AVOID CONTACT WITH EYES. IF THIS OCCURS, RINSE THOROUGELY WITH WATER. KEEP OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children.

## Directions
