# Phenol and Zinc Oxide

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 5

**Also sold as:** Calamine Phenolated Topical Suspension, Foster and Thrive Phenolated Calamine, Unguentine, Sunmark Phenolated Calamine

## Active ingredients

Calamine 8% Active Ingredient Zinc Oxide 8% Active Ingredient Liquefied Phenol

## Purpose

Skin Protectant Purpose Skin Protectant Purpose Topical Analgesic

## Uses

Dries the oozing and weeping and temporarily pain and itching of poison ivy, poison oak, and poison sumac, or other minor skin irritations

## Warnings

For external use only . Use only as directed. Avoid contact with eyes and mucous membranes. Do not apply to large areas of the body or in large quantities, particularly over raw or blistered areas. If applied to fingers or toes do not bandage.

### Ask a doctor before use if

Ask a doctor before using on children under 2 years of age. When using this product. Discontinue use if condition worsen or does not improve within 7 days and consult a doctor.

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children. In case of accidental ingestion, seek professional assistance or contact a poison Control Center immediately.

## Directions

(Shake well before using) Adult and children 2 years of age and older: Shake well before using. Cleanse the skin with soap and water and let dry before each use. Apply product to the affected area using cotton or soft cloth, not more than 3 to 4 times daily as needed for comfort. Children under 2 years of age : Consult a doctor before use.

## Inactive ingredients

. Bentonite Magma, Calcium Hydroxide, Glycerin, Purified Water.
