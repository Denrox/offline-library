## Purpose

1. Helps to quickly and effectively remove excess skin tags on the skin and significantly improve skin condition. 2. It can restore skin to a smooth and healthy state, enhance your confidence and improve overall appearance. 3. It uses mild ingredients, is not irritating to the skin, and is suitable for all types of skin.

## Uses

1. Clean skin and keep it dry 2. Take an appropriate amount of this product and apply it evenly on the affected skin or skin tags 3. Gently massage until completely absorbed.
