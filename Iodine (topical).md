# Iodine (topical)

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 9

**Also sold as:** Iodo Blanco (iodine Tincture Decolorized) First Aid Antiseptic, Iodine Tincture, Ioddo Iodine, Iodine Tincture (iodo) First Aid Antiseptic, Salicylic Iodine (iodo Salicilico) First Aid Antiseptic

## Active ingredients

Iodine 2%

## Purpose

First aid antiseptic

## Uses

First aid to help prevent skin infection in minor cuts scrapes burns

## Warnings

For external use only

### Ask a doctor before use if

you have deep or puncture wounds, animal bites or serious burns

### When using this product

do not use in eyes or apply over large areas of the body do not use longer than 1 week unless directed by a doctor

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

condition persists or gets worse

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

## Directions

clean the affected area apply a small amount on the area 1 to 3 times daily may be covered with a sterile bandage if bandaged, let dry first

## Inactive ingredients

alcohol (47% v/v), purified water, sodium iodide
