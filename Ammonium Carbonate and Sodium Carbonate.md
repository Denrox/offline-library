# Ammonium Carbonate and Sodium Carbonate

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** respiratory (inhalation)  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Best Smelling Salts

## Active ingredients

Ammonium Carbonate 30% Sodium Carbonate 70%

## Purpose

Ammonia Compound

## Uses

Add tablespoon of water and shake

## Warnings

For external use only Avoid contact with eyes and skin

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children

## Directions

For temporary use as a smelling salt Use as directed

## Other information

Store at room temperature
