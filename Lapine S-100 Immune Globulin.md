**Also sold as:** Spirudio

## Active ingredients

Active: Targeted antibodies to S100 protein (helps regulate mood, focus, and stress response).

## Purpose

May help enhance: Mental clarity Concentration and attention Emotional stability May help reduce: Feelings of anxiety and stress Restlessness and irritability Impulsivity and emotional reactivity This natural formula may help you feel calmer, more focused, and better equipped to handle the demands of everyday life while supporting long-term mental well-being.

## Uses

Directions: Dissolve 1 tablet, three times daily under the tongue. For stronger effects, up to 2 tablets, three times daily may be taken. Use regularly for best results.

## Warnings

If pregnant or breastfeeding, ask a healthcare professional before use. If symptoms persist or worsen, discontinue use and consult a healthcare professional. Keep out of reach of children. Do not use if tamper-evident seal is broken or missing. Consult a physician before use if you have a medical condition or are taking any medication. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
