# Potassium Nitrate (dental)

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** dental  
**Labels on file:** 4

**Also sold as:** Spring Gene Oil Pulling Sensitivity, Sprinjene Natural Sensitivity and Gum, Boka Sensitive, Sensitivity Relief Fluoride Free

## Active ingredients

Potassium Nitrate 5%

## Purpose

Antihypersensitivity

## Uses

Helps reduce painful sensitivity of the teeth to cold, heat, acids, and sweets, or contact.

## Warnings

Keep out of reach of children. Sensitive teeth may indicate a serious problem that may need prompt care by a dentist. See your dentist if the problem persists or worsens. Do not use this product longer than 4 weeks unless recommended by a dentist or doctor.

## Directions

Adults and children 12 years of age and older: Apply at least a 1-inch strip of the product onto a soft-bristle toothbrush. Brush teeth thoroughly for at least 1 minute twice a day (morning and evening) or as recommended by a dentist or doctor. Make sure to brush all sensitive areas of the teeth. Children under 12 years of age: Consult a dentist or doctor.
