# Rig Expert AA-35 ZOOM Amateur Band coverage in the United States

*Tags: hf, antenna-analyzer · score 3*

## Question

I am having difficulty understanding the low end of the AA-35 ZOOM frequency range. The tool can help with frequencies between 35 and .06 MHz. Looking at a chart I saw that 60 kHz was Standard Frequency and Time Signal. This area on the frequency charts seems to be below 2,200 Meters. My question is this; does the AA-35 ZOOM measure below 2,200 Meters? If so, what is the application for United States Amateur Radio users? My understanding is that the AA-35 ZOOM covers 160-10 Meters.

## Answer (score 4)

60 kHz is about 5000 meters in wave length so yes it does go below 2,200 meters.

The Rig Expert AA-35 is an antenna and cable analyzer. While often used by amateur radio operators, it is not restricted to them. For example it could be used to measure an antenna for commercial AM radio which in the US ranges from 540 Khz to 1700 kHz. It covers a large range of frequencies and impedances. Many of which are not used in amateur radio.
