# Sulfur (cutaneous)

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** cutaneous  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Sulfursoap

## Purpose

Indications · For the treatment of acne. · Helps to reduce pimples and blackheads. · Promotes clearer skin by reducing excess oil production. usage section · Wet skin and apply a thin layer of soap to the affected area. · Gently massage and lather. · Rinse thoroughly with water. · Use twice daily, morning and night, or as directed by a healthcare provider.
