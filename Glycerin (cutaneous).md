### Ask a doctor before use if

Ask Doctor If you have a history of skin allergies or have sensitive skin, it is advisable to consult a doctor before using this product.

### When using this product

Avoid contact with eyes and broken skin. If redness, swelling, or irritation occurs, discontinue use immediately and seek medical advice.
