## Active ingredients

APIS MELLIFERA VENOM、HONEY、ARTEMISIA VULGARIS ROOT

## Purpose

Effectively softens and removes warts; relieves skin discomfort and promotes painless shedding; soothes and protects the skin, gently removes warts.

## Uses

1. Clean the skin. 2. Apply an appropriate amount of this product evenly on the skin of the required parts of the body.

## Warnings

Please keep out of reach of children. Do not swallow.Please clean your hands before use to ensure the best results from the product. Discontinue use if signs of irritation or rash occur. Store in a cool and dry place.
