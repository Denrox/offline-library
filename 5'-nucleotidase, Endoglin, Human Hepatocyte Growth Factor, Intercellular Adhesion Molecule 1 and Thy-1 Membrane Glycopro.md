**Route:** topical

**Also sold as:** Cpc Chondrocyte Preparation

## Active ingredients

CD73: 0.90 mg/mL CD90: 0.90 mg/mL CD105: 0.90 mg/mL ICAM-1: 0.70 mg/mL HGF: 0.50 mg/mL

## Purpose

Active protein-factor suspension for topical / local external use

## Uses

For topical / local external use; apply only to the target local area according to labeling directions.

## Warnings

For topical / local external use only; do not ingest, inject, or apply to eyes, mucous membranes, or broken skin; single use only. Ask a doctor before use if you have active skin infection, open wounds, severe skin disease, known allergy to protein-based ingredients, or are pregnant or breastfeeding. Ask a doctor or pharmacist before use if you are using other topical medicines, anti-allergy medicines, or immune-related treatments. Do not use if the package is damaged or leaking; the storage temperature has deviated from the required range; the suspension appears abnormally turbid, discolored, or contains visible foreign matter; the product has been frozen or repeatedly frozen and thawed; or the labeled expiration date has passed. Stop use and ask a doctor if rash, redness, swelling, itching, irritation, hypersensitivity, or any other discomfort occurs. When using this product avoid contact with eyes, mucous membranes, and broken skin; avoid vigorous shaking; do not freeze or refreeze; use immediately after opening as a single-use vial. Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
