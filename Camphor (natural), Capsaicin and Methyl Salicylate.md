# Camphor (natural), Capsaicin and Methyl Salicylate

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 2

**Also sold as:** Zourunan Pain Relief

## Active ingredients

Methyl salicylate 30% Capsaicin 0.025% Camphor 5%

## Purpose

Topical analgesic

## Uses

Temporarily relieves muscle and stiffness due to:

- minor injuries
- overexertion
- falls reduces symptoms of bruising such as
- pain
- swelling
- discoloration
