## Active ingredients

Sodium Monofluorophosphate: 1.14% w/w (1,500 ppm fluoride as added) (0.15% w/w fluoride ion)

## Purpose

Anti-cavity

## Uses

Helps protect against cavities

## Warnings

AND PRECAUTIONS SECTION Children of six years and younger: Use a pea-sized amount for supervised brushing to minimize swallowing. In case of intake of fluoride from other sources, consult a dentist or doctor.

### Keep out of reach of children

Warning Keep out of reach of children

## Directions

Brush thoroughly, preferably after each meal or at least twice a day. Do not swallow. Rinse and spit out after use.

## Inactive ingredients

Sorbitol, Water, Hydrated Silica, Sodium Lauryl Sulfate, Flavor, PEG-12, Cellulose Gum, Sodium Saccharin, Methylparaben, Mica, Propylparaben, FD&C Red No.40
