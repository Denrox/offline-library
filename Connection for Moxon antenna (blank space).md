# Connection for Moxon antenna (blank space)

*Tags: antenna, antenna-construction · score 3*

## Question

I just printed a Moxon Antenna and want to wire it. The Antenna wiring has a blank space and I'm not sure how to wire this place. From picture bellow I got that I need to connect a coax shield to top's one arm and conductor to another.

But both arms has 17mm space where I can't fit the wire. What is this space? When I just insert wire to as shown at the picture wire will not be connected to coax cable, is it okay?

## Accepted answer (score 7, by Louis Seaman)

There is no connection in the 17mm space. The wire that sits in the 46x249x46 space is simply a "reflector", and has no connection to the other wires, which are considered the "driven" elements (driven by the connection to your two conductors coming from your transmitter).
