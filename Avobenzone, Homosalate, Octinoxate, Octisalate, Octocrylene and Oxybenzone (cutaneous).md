# Avobenzone, Homosalate, Octinoxate, Octisalate, Octocrylene and Oxybenzone (cutaneous)

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** cutaneous  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Skintific Outdoor Sun Broad Spectrum Spf 50

## Active ingredients

HOMOSALATE 15% OCTOCRYLENE 10% OCTINOXATE 7.4% OXYBENZONE 4% AVOBENZONE 3%

## Purpose

SUNSCREEN

## Uses

- Helps prevent sunburn
- If used as directed with other sun protection measures (see Directions) , decreases the risk of skin cancer and early skin aging caused by the sun.

## Warnings

- For external use only.
- Do not use on damaged or broken skin.
- Stop use and ask a doctor if rash occurs
- When using this product keep out of eyes. Rinse with water to remove
- Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away. Flammable: Keep away from fire or flame.
- Avoid spraying in eyes. Contents under pressure. Do not puncture or incinerate. Do not store at temperature above 120 °F.

## Directions

- Apply liberally 15 minutes before sun exposure.
- Shake well before use.
- Do not spray directly into face. Spray on hands then apply to face.
- Apply to all skin exposed to the sun
- Reapply at least every 2 hours.
- Use a water-resistant sunscreen if swimming or sweating.
- Children under 6 months: Ask a doctor.

## Other information

- store at controlled room temperature 20°-25°C (68°-77°F). Protect from freezing and excessive heat over 40°C (104°F).
