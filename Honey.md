HONEY EXTRACT

## Purpose

1. It can quickly relieve eye dryness and fatigue and restore eye comfort. 2. Contains plant ingredients and has a cool texture, which can give you a refreshing and comfortable experience. 3. Compact packaging design, easy to carry around, providing eye care anytime and anywhere.

## Uses

1. Open the lid and raise your head 45 degrees. 2. Put 1-2 drops of eye drops into each eye. 3. Close your eyes until it is absorbed. 4.Finally, wipe off excess liquid with a paper towel.

## Warnings
