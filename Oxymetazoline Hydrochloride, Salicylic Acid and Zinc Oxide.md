## Purpose

Antiseptic, Hand Sanitizer

## Uses

Hand Sanitizer to help reduce bacteria that potentially can cause disease. For use when soap and water are not available.

## Warnings

For external use only. Flammable. Keep away from heat or flame

### Do not use

in children less than 2 months of age on open skin wounds

### When using this product

keep out of eyes, ears, and mouth. In case of contact with eyes, rinse eyes thoroughly with water. Stop use and ask a doctor if irritation or rash occurs. These may be signs of a serious condition. Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

## Directions

Place enough product on hands to cover all surfaces. Rub hands together until dry. Supervise children under 6 years of age when using this product to avoid swallowing.

## Other information
