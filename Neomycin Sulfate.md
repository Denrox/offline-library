## Warnings

When handling this product

- May cause skin & eye irritation; wear full PPE (gloves, goggles, respirator)
- Avoid direct skin contact, inhalation of dust
- Do not ingest; keep separate from food, drinks
- Keep away from ignition sources, strong oxidizers
- Restless/irritability may occur with improper exposure, especially for personnel without chemical handling training If pregnant/breastfeeding, consult industrial hygiene specialist before handling. Keep out of reach of children & untrained personnel. In case of accidental ingestion/skin absorption/eye contact, flush affected area with plenty of clean water and seek emergency medical help immediately.
