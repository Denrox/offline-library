# What is this BNC-type radio connector about 13mm diameter?

*Tags: antenna, connectors · score 4*

## Question

What is this radio connector? LIKE BNC, but it is about 13mm in diameter, odd.

Follow up: It turns out putting a sma BNC adapter in the center of it does work. In place of a "C" or "N" type to BNC, could I just use jbweld or some sturdy glue around this instead of holding it with the center tap in the center tap of the adapter?

## Answer (score 7, by mike65535)

That looks like a Type-C RF connector.
