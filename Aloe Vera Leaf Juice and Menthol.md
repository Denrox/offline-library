# Aloe Vera Leaf Juice and Menthol

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Reduce Lip Lines

## Active ingredients

ACTIVE INGEREDIENT SECTION Menthol (from Peppermint Oil) 0.4% Aloe Vera Gel 13%

## Purpose

Temporarily relieves pain from chapped lips,Forms a protective barrier to heal dry/cracked lips,Enhances lip elasticity and reduces visible wrinkles,Brightens lip tone and evens pigmentation

## Uses

Reduce Lip Lines Relieve & Repair Increase Elasticity Lip Plumping Brightening

## Warnings

If allergic to mint, cinnamon, or botanical oils,On bleeding or infected lip areas,In children under 2 years

### When using this product

Avoid contact with eyes. If contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water Discontinue if burning sensation persists over 10 minutes Not for ingestion. If swallowed, seek medical help immediately

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

If allergic to mint, cinnamon, or botanical oils On bleeding or infected lip areas In children under 2 years

### Keep out of reach of children

If ingested, contact Poison Control Center

## Directions

Apply thin layer to clean lips using fingertip Gently massage for 30 seconds to enhance absorption Use up to 4 times daily, preferably at bedtime For overnight repair: Apply thick layer before sleep

## Other information

Keep tightly closed at 15-30°C (59-86°F). Avoid heat above 40°C (104°F) and direct sunlight.

## Inactive ingredients

Candelilla Wax, Coconut Oil, Chamomile Extract, Cinnamon Oil, ROSA CANINA SEED OIL, Hyaluronic Acid, Jojoba Oil, Tocopherol, Vanilla Extract, Grapeseed Extract
