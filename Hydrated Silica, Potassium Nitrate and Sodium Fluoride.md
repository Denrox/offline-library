# Hydrated Silica, Potassium Nitrate and Sodium Fluoride

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** oral  
**Labels on file:** 1

**Also sold as:** Rorobell Wawa Sensitive Teeth Tooth

## Active ingredients

Hydrated Silica 18.5% Potassium Nitrate 5.0% Sodium Fluoride 0.3152%

## Purpose

Anti-plaque Protection of sensitive teeth Anti-cavity

## Uses

- Helps keep teeth white and strong.
- Helps keep the oral cavity clean.
- Helps freshen the oral cavity.
- Helps eliminate bad breath.
- Helps improve the appearance of teeth.
- Helps prevent and relieve tooth sensitivity.
- Helps remove dental plaque (anti-plaque).
- Helps prevent cavities with fluoride.

## Warnings

Do not use this product

- Do not use this product if you are hypersensitive or allergic to any of its ingredients. When using this product
- Use this product only for brushing teeth. Stop use and ask a physician, dentist, or pharmacist if Bring the product information with you whenever possible when seeking medical advice.
- Irritation develops after using this product.
- Swelling of the mouth or face occurs. This may be a sign of a very rare allergic reaction. Other warnings
- This toothpaste contains 1,426 ppm fluoride.
- Because this toothpaste contains potassium nitrate, consult a physician or dentist before use in children under 12 years of age.
- Do not swallow this toothpaste. Rinse your mouth thoroughly after use.
- Children under 6 years of age should use only a pea-sized amount of toothpaste under adult supervision to prevent sucking or swallowing the toothpaste.
- If a child under 6 years of age swallows a large amount of this toothpaste, consult a physician or dentist immediately.
- Tooth sensitivity may indicate an underlying condition that requires prompt treatment. If symptoms persist or worsen, consult a dentist.
- Because this toothpaste contains more than 1,000 ppm fluoride, consult a physician or dentist before use in children under 6 years of age.
- As with other oral care products, discontinue use if irritation develops. Storage
- Keep out of reach of children under 6 years of age.
- Store this product in its original container with the cap tightly closed. Do not transfer it to another container, as doing so may result in accidental misuse or reduce the quality of the product.

## Directions

- Brush teeth twice daily by applying an appropriate amount of toothpaste to a toothbrush.
