SOPHORA ANGUSTIFOLIA ROOT EXTRACT, ALOE BARBADENSIS EXTRACT

## Purpose

Nail Regeneration Reduces Discoloration Improves Cracked & Brittle Nails Removes & Prevents Nail Fungus Smooths And Hydrates Nail

## Uses

Wash and dry affected nails before going to bed. Open the package, take out the patch and tear off the back film. Place the patch on the nail and remove it after about 6-8 hours.
