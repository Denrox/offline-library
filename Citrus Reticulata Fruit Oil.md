## Active ingredients

CITRUS RETICULATA (TANGERINE) FRUIT EXTRACT

## Purpose

Can block body odor throughout the day and continuously control odors for 72 hours. Suitable for odor control in pits, private areas, and other areasthink of feet, under the chest, navel, abdominal folds, hip cracks, thigh creases, or any other areas where you have an odor.

## Uses

1. Firstly, clean the area that needs to be used 2. Open the cover, gently rotate the bottom shaft, and then push out the rod. And directly apply it to the area that needs to be used
