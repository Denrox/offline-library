## Active ingredients

Pramoxine HCl 1% Zinc Acetate 0.1%

## Purpose

Topical analgesic Skin protectant

## Uses

temporarily relieves pain and itching associated with: rashes due to posion ivy, poison oak or poison sumac insect bites minor skin irritation minor cuts dries the oozing and weeping of poison ivy, poison oak and poison sumac

## Warnings

For external use only Flammable: Do not use while smoking or near heat or flame Do not apply to wounds or damaged, broken or irritated skin. do not allow contact with eyes or mucous membranes. When using this product keep out of eyes (Rinse with water to remove) do not puncture or incinerate. Contents under pressure. do not store at temperatures above 120 degrees F. Stop use and ask a doctor if condition worsens or does not improve within 7 days symptoms persist for more than 7 days or clear up and occur again within a few days If pregnant or breast-feeding ask a health professional before use. Keep out of reach of children and pets. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
