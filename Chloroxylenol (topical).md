## Active ingredients

Chloroxylenol 1.75 % w/w

## Purpose

Antiseptic

## Uses

To decrease bacteria on the skin that could potentially cause disease.

## Warnings

For external use only. When using this product avoid contact with eyes. If contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water. Stop use and ask a doctor if irritation or redness develops. KEEP OUT OF REACH OF CHILDREN. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
