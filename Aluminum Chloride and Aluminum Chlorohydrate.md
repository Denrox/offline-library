## Uses

Reduces underarm perspiration.

## Warnings

For external use only. Do not use on irritated or damaged skin if you have shaved within the last 6 hours in combination with other deodorants Stop use and ask a doctor if rash or irritation occurs Ask a doctor before use if you have kidney disease Keep out of the reach of children If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away. Avoid contact with eyes or mucous membranes.

## Directions

Apply to underarm only. In the evening, before going to bed, wash armpits and dry thoroughly. Spray 2-3 times in each armpit and allow to dry. Wait until dry before dressing. For best effect let it work overnight. Shower or wash in the morning as usual. Use again only when its effect wears off!
