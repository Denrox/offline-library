# Carboxymethylcellulose Sodium, Unspecified (topical)

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** topical  
**Labels on file:** 2

**Also sold as:** Auro-cmc, Carboxymethylcellulose Sodium Eye Drops 0.5%

## Active ingredients

Carboxymethyl cellulose sodium Eye Drops 0.5% w/v

## Purpose

Eye lubricant Eye lubricant

## Uses

For use as a lubricant to prevent further irritation or to relieve dryness of the eye Indication & usage Do not touch the nozzle tip to any surface since this may contaminate the solution Remove contact lenses before use Should not use at the same time as other ophthalmic drugs Replace cap after using

## Warnings

For external use only

### Do not use

1.If you are sensitive to any ingredient in this product 2.If solution changes color or becomes cloudy

### Ask a doctor before use if

Stop use and ask a doctor if 1.Excessive watering of the eye 2.Burning, stinging,foreign body sensation Continued redness (or) irritation of the eye, unusual eye secretions, change in vision

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children If swallowed get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away

## Directions

Dose Instill 1 or 2 drops in the affected eyes as needed

## Inactive ingredients

1.Boric acid 2.Calcium chloride 3.Glycerin 4.Magnesium chloride 5.Mannitol 6.Potassium chloride 7.Purified water 8.Stablized oxy cholro complex 9.Sodium tetra borate 10.Sodium hyaluronate 11.Sodium citrate
