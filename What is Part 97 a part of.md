# What is Part 97 a part of?

*Tags: united-states, legal · score 6*

## Question

The regulations that govern Amateur Radio in the United States are often described as Part 97.

What statute are they a part of? Where can I find other parts of the same law?

## Accepted answer (score 6, by Nick ODell - KD0OCR)

The full name is 47 CFR (Code of Federal Regulations) 97.

You can find other parts that radio transmissions are regulated by on the FCC's website.
