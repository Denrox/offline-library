# Sodium Chloride (ophthalmic)

> **Note:** Official U.S. label text. Doses and directions differ between products and strengths (for example adult tablets and children's liquids): always follow the label of the product you have. Not medical advice; ask a pharmacist or doctor if unsure.

**Route:** ophthalmic  
**Labels on file:** 14

**Also sold as:** Muro 128, Walgreens, Sodium Chloride Hypertonicity, Sodium Chloride Hypertonicity Ophthalmic Solution, 5%, Sodium Chloride Ophthalmic 5 Percent Hypertonicity Eye, Walgreens Sodium Chloride Ophthalmic 5 Percent Hypertonicity Eye, Altachlore Sodium Chloride Hypertonicity, Altachlore Sodium Chloride Hypertonicity Ophthalmic 5 Percent

## Active ingredients

Sodium chloride 5%

## Purpose

Hypertoncity agent Uses: temporary relief of corneal edema

## Uses

Directions pull down the lower lid of the affected eye

## Warnings

For external use only.

### Do not use

except under the advice and supervision of a doctor

### When using this product

it may cause temporary burning and irritation replace cap after use to avoid contamination, do not touch tip of container to any surface

### Stop use and ask a doctor if

condition worsens or persists for more than 72 hours you experience eye pain, changes in vision, continued redness or irritation of the eye

### Keep out of reach of children

Keep out of reach of children If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

## Directions

apply a small amount (1/4 inch) of ointment to the inside of the eyelid apply every 3 or 4 hours or as directed by a doctor

## Inactive ingredients

Lanolin, Mineral Oil, Purified Water, White Petrolatum.
